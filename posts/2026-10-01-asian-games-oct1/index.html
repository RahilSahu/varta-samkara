INDIA vs PAKISTAN in an Asian Games cricket final. Let that sink in.

October 1 was a blockbuster day for India at the Asian Games 2026 in Aichi-Nagoya:

- CRICKET: India posted 169/7 (Ishan Kishan 80 not out off 46) and bowled Sri Lanka out for 45. Won by 124 runs. Gold-medal match against Pakistan on Saturday. - HOCKEY: India beat Pakistan 4-3 in a thriller. Led 3-0, Pakistan drew level 3-3, then Abhishek scored in the 60th minute to seal the final. India defend their title. - WRESTLING: Nitesh Siwach won silver in men's Greco-Roman 97kg, losing 0-4 to Japan's Yuri Nakazato. First Indian in a Greco-Roman final in 64 years. - Medal tally: 63 medals with 10 golds.
