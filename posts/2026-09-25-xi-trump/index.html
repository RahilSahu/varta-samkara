XI JINPING IN WASHINGTON, EXPLAINED

China's President Xi Jinping met Donald Trump at the White House on Sep 24, in his first US state visit since 2015.

What we know: 1. The three-day visit began Sep 23 with a rare planeside welcome at Joint Base Andrews, the first in 11 years. It was the leaders' second meeting this year after the May Beijing summit. 2. Trade: both sides extended the Busan agreement to Jan 10, pausing a trade war where mutual tariffs topped 100%. Tariffs lowered, rare-earth export controls eased, and China is moving on a 200-Boeing-jet order. 3. AI: Trump said 'super intelligence' would be a big topic. Both sides discussed AI risks, but US tech export controls stay. Xi pressed on Taiwan arms; Iran was discussed. The state dinner hosted Musk, Cook, Bezos, Pichai, Altman and Huang.
