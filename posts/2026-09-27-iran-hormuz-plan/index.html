TRUMP REJECTS IRAN'S 7-DAY PLAN TO REOPEN THE STRAIT OF HORMUZ

Iran proposed a seven-day roadmap at the UN General Assembly: the US would release frozen Iranian assets, lift sanctions on Iranian oil and end its naval blockade of Iranian ports, in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and a regional ceasefire including Lebanon.

Under the plan, presented through Qatari mediators on Sept 25, US steps would take 4-5 days, the strait would reopen by day 6-7, and talks on a final deal would begin on day 7.

On Sept 26, Trump rejected it publicly: "I reject their proposal... They want a deal to open the Hormuz Strait immediately because they're losing so badly."

The Wall Street Journal reports Trump is privately skeptical Tehran will meet his demands and expects a renewed US bombing campaign after the November midterms. Iran says it is still waiting for a formal reply through the mediators.

The sticking point: Trump demands Iran permanently abandon any path to a nuclear weapon, while Iran says it will show no flexibility on its nuclear program, including uranium enrichment.
