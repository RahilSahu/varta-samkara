OPINION | Ancient Hindu traditions were surprisingly eco-friendly.

Clay idols that dissolved back into rivers. Offerings of milk, grain, and flowers that fed fish, birds, and insects instead of becoming waste. Haldi, kumkum, and sandalwood instead of chemical colours. Earthen lamps instead of plastic decor.

The core principle was simple: take from nature, use responsibly, and return without harm.

Much of today's festival pollution comes not from tradition, but from modern replacements: Plaster of Paris idols, plastic decorations, synthetic colours, and commercialized display.

Faith was meant to connect humans with nature.
