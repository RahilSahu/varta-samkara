"A terrorist country is asking me that question."

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar shut down a Pakistani journalist at the UN headquarters in New York on Sept 25, when he was asked: "When will your government stop sponsoring terrorism in Pakistan?"

The exchange happened at a joint press briefing with Liberia's foreign minister, where the two launched a new Group of Friends on the Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers.

India's Permanent Representative to the UN, Harish Parvathaneni, backed the minister: "You have heard your reply. You have heard your response, loud and clear."

A day earlier, an ANI reporter had asked Pakistan's PM Shehbaz Sharif, "When will you stop harbouring terrorists?" He walked past without answering.
