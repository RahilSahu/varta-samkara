"A terrorist country is asking me that question."

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar delivered a sharp rebuttal to a Pakistani journalist at the United Nations headquarters in New York on September 25, after being asked when India would stop "sponsoring terrorism" in Pakistan.

"A terrorist country is asking me that question," Jaishankar said, cutting off the line of questioning during a joint press briefing with Liberia's foreign minister. The two ministers were at the UN to launch a new Group of Friends on the Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers.

Backing from India's UN envoy

India's Permanent Representative to the UN, Harish Parvathaneni, stood firmly behind the minister. "You have heard your reply. You have heard your response, loud and clear," he told the journalist, closing the exchange.

The confrontation did not come out of nowhere. A day earlier, an ANI reporter had posed a mirror-image question to Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, asking him when Pakistan would stop harbouring terrorists. Sharif walked past without answering.

The exchange, which took place on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly session, quickly drew attention back home, with Jaishankar's response being widely shared across Indian media and social platforms.
