HCLTech just bought a Croatian AI company. Here is what the 9-million-euro deal is really about.

HCLSoftware, HCLTech's software division, will acquire 100% of Robotiq.ai, an enterprise robotic process automation company based in Zagreb, Croatia.

The all-cash deal is valued at 9 million euros (about USD 10.5 million) and is expected to close in November 2026. It goes through HCL Technologies Austria GmbH, a step-down wholly owned subsidiary of HCLTech.

Robotiq.ai's software automates repetitive, manual processes across business applications, even where APIs are unavailable or inadequate. Its customers include large banks, insurance companies and telecom providers.

HCLSoftware says the acquisition adds execution muscle to its HCL UnO Agentic platform: AI agents move from decision-making to actually completing work inside enterprise apps.

Founded in August 2018, Robotiq.ai reported revenue of 1.4 million euros and a profit of 0.2 million euros in 2025.
