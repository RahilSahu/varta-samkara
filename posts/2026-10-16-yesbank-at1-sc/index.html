SC STRIKES DOWN YES BANK'S Rs 8,415 CRORE AT1 BOND WRITE-OFF

The Supreme Court on Oct 15 upheld the Bombay High Court's 2023 ruling quashing YES Bank's write-off of Additional Tier-1 (AT1) bonds worth Rs 8,415 crore during its 2020 financial crisis.

A bench led by Justice Dipankar Datta said there was no reason to interfere with the High Court's decision. The court dismissed appeals by the Finance Ministry, RBI and YES Bank, and vacated its stay on the judgment.

The write-off was held "illegal and arbitrary" as it went against the Final Reconstruction Scheme gazetted by the Union of India. The Bombay High Court had found the administrator exceeded his authority by writing off the bonds after the bank was reconstructed on March 13, 2020.

AT1 bonds are high-return debt instruments that can be written off or converted to equity in a crisis. The ruling gives bondholders, including mutual funds and retail investors, a pathway to recover their investments. YES Bank shares crashed up to 20% after the verdict.

The Centre had argued the write-off protected over 2 lakh depositors and warned of "deleterious consequences" for the banking system. Experts say the ruling creates regulatory uncertainty for future bank resolutions.
