US sending 3rd aircraft carrier to Middle East as Trump weighs fresh Iran strikes

The US is dispatching its biggest naval buildup in the region since 2003: the USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier strike group is sailing for the Middle East, joined by the USS Makin Island group carrying 2,000 Marines of the 13th MEU. Up to 10,000 more troops in total are expected to arrive by the end of November, per US officials.

Why now: indirect US-Iran talks at the UN ended in stalemate, and Trump says Iran must "sign a very fair deal, or they won't exist any longer." Washington has also rolled out fresh sanctions on Iran's auto and rail sectors. Brent crude jumped 4.4% to $102.31 a barrel on Oct 1 as at least 3 tankers were attacked this week in the Strait of Hormuz, a route most of India's oil imports pass through.
