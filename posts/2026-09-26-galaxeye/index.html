India is funding a startup building a satellite that can see through clouds and darkness.

On 24 September 2026, the Technology Development Board (under the Department of Science and Technology) signed an agreement with Bengaluru-based GalaxEye Space Solutions.

The deal: Rs 63.84 crore from the Centre's RDI Fund toward a Rs 247.69 crore project.

The satellite will use OptoSAR imaging, combining radar (SAR) and optical (EO) sensors on one platform. That means resolution sharper than 0.5 metres, working at night and through cloud cover.

Applications include wide-area surveillance, human and vehicle detection, and target classification. The project moves the technology from TRL-6 (demonstrated prototype) to TRL-9 (proven in operation).
