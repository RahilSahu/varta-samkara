India just had its worst monsoon in 11 years.

The IMD says the 2026 southwest monsoon ended with 759.4 mm of rain, only 87% of the long-period average. A deficit of nearly 13%, the driest season since 2015 and the fourth driest this century.

June was 35% below normal, August 16.3% short, September 7.6% short. Only July was normal.

East and Northeast India had their driest monsoon since 1901. The South Peninsula got 542 mm, its fourth lowest since 1901.

282 of 741 districts ended deficient. Punjab, Assam, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh were short in all four months.

El Nino is blamed, and it is still strengthening: it is expected to peak in November and fade by March 2027. October rainfall is forecast below normal, with hotter days across most of India.
