Union Cabinet's 3 big decisions: Rabi MSP hike, Rs 1.86 lakh cr green grid, AI traffic plan for Delhi

On Sep 30, 2026, the Union Cabinet approved three major measures: higher Minimum Support Prices for all six mandated Rabi crops for the 2027-28 marketing season (safflower up Rs 675 to Rs 7,215/quintal, wheat up Rs 25 to Rs 2,610), the Rs 1,86,405 crore PM-DHARA (Green Energy Corridor Phase-III) scheme to evacuate up to 135 GW of renewables with 50 GWh of battery storage by FY 2032-33, and a Rs 1,789.52 crore Intelligent Traffic Management System for Delhi covering 42 corridors in 3 phases. Total outlay: Rs 2,79,157 crore. Announced by Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw.
