India just named its next Air Chief.

Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit will be the 29th Chief of the Air Staff, taking charge as Air Chief Marshal on October 31, 2026, when Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh superannuates. His tenure runs till December 31, 2028.

A fighter pilot of nearly four decades, Dixit has logged over 3,500 flying hours as an experimental test pilot and qualified flying instructor, flying the Mirage-2000, MiG variants, Jaguar, Tejas, Hawk, Kiran, An-32 and IL-78.

He has served in Operation Sindoor, Operation Safed Sagar and Operation Rakshak, commanded a frontline fighter base in the Western Sector, led Central Air Command, and was Chief of Integrated Defence Staff to the Chairman, Chiefs of Staff Committee.

He holds the Param Vishisht Seva Medal (2026), Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (2023), Vishisht Seva Medal (2011) and Vayu Sena Medal (2006).
