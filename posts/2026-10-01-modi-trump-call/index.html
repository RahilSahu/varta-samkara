Modi just got off the phone with Trump. Here is what the two leaders discussed.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held a telephone conversation with US President Donald Trump on Wednesday. The two leaders reviewed bilateral cooperation across trade, defence, energy and critical technologies.

The PMO said both leaders reaffirmed their commitment to advancing the India-US Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership and agreed to maintain close engagement. They also exchanged views on regional and international developments, including ongoing efforts to promote global peace and security.

Modi called the call "productive" on X. US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor called it "a very good call".

The timing matters. The conversation comes as the US enacted a sanctions law allowing tariffs of up to 100% on nations buying Russian oil and gas, including India. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal is in the United States till October 5, pushing for an interim trade deal on the road to a full bilateral trade agreement.
