Modi's Mann Ki Baat: 10 years of surgical strikes, census duty, and a solar village

PM Modi aired the 138th episode of Mann Ki Baat on Sunday, marking 10 years of the 2016 surgical strikes across the LoC.

He reiterated India's zero-tolerance policy on terrorism, citing Balakot and Operation Sindoor: "if terrorists dare to cast an evil eye on India, they will be met with a befitting response."

Modi called the Census a national responsibility: 32+ crore families recorded in the first phase, 2.6+ crore people self-enumerating online in 16 languages, taking 15-20 minutes with no document upload.

He urged a Swachh Bharat push ahead of Gandhi Jayanti, calling cleanliness "the most beautiful tribute we can pay to Bapu," and hailed Modhera's rise from historic Sun Temple town to India's first solar village.
