JAPAN PROTESTS AFTER US MARINE ARRESTED IN OKINAWA

A 20-year-old US Marine has been arrested on suspicion of robbery and murder in Okinawa, prompting a strong diplomatic protest from Tokyo.

Devin J. Ballard, a lance corporal stationed at Marine Corps Air Station Futenma with the III Marine Expeditionary Force, was arrested on Oct 4. Police allege he killed Anna Yagi, 39, at a hotel in Naha in the early hours of Oct 3; she died of suffocation and her wallet and backpack were taken. Ballard denies the charges.

PM Sanae Takaichi called it an "extremely brutal and heinous incident" and lodged a strong protest. Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi summoned US Ambassador George Glass, and the defense minister protested to the US Forces Japan commander. The US side offered condolences and pledged full cooperation.
