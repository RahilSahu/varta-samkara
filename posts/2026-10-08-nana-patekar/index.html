BREAKING: Bollywood has lost one of its greatest. Nana Patekar is no more.

The veteran actor, 75, died this morning at his home in Dona Paula, Goa, after a cardiac arrest.

He was found unconscious and rushed to Manipal Hospital, where doctors attempted CPR for 45 minutes but could not revive him. He was declared dead around 5:10 am.

Dr Shekhar Salkar of Manipal Hospital told ANI a medico-legal case has been registered and a post-mortem will be conducted.

Patekar won three National Film Awards and the Padma Shri (2013). He is remembered for Parinda, Krantiveer, Ab Tak Chhappan, Welcome and Natsamrat. His last public appearance was on Oct 4 in Dona Paula.

Om Shanti. Follow for verified news.
