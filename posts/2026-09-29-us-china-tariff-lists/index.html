US AND CHINA RELEASE $30B TARIFF-CUT LISTS

Days after the Trump-Xi summit in Washington, the US and China released reciprocal lists of products that will get tariff cuts, about $30 billion each way.

The lists cover 77 Chinese goods (fireworks, Christmas ornaments, soccer balls, toys, microwave ovens, fish hooks) and 1,619 US products (poultry, dairy, peanuts, noodles, silk, breeding horses, timber, medical equipment, infant formula, coal).

Over 90% of the listed products will drop to standard most-favored-nation tariff levels, effectively wiping out country-specific tariffs on them. The wider trade truce, which was set to expire on Nov 10, has been extended to January.

USTR Jamieson Greer says the deal improves market access for about 30% of US exports to China. But chips, EVs and batteries are not covered, and analysts caution the overall impact may be limited. Two-way trade was $495 billion in 2025, down 25% year on year. Next up: APEC in Shenzhen in November and G20 in Miami in December.
