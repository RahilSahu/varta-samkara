📰 A US judge just blocked Trump's White House press ban.

A federal judge has blocked the Trump administration's ban on press access for three news organisations, ordering the White House to immediately restore their credentials.

US District Judge Timothy Kelly issued a 14-day order directing the administration to reinstate press passes for CNN, MS NOW and Politico, ruling that the ban likely violated the outlets' due process rights. The order rejected the White House's national security justification for revoking access.

Access restored after chaotic morning

The White House reinstated the credentials on Thursday, following what reporters described as a chaotic morning of continued denials even after the court ruling was issued. The administration has been ordered to keep the passes active while the case proceeds.

The Justice Department has said it will appeal the ruling, setting up a further legal battle over the White House's authority to restrict press access. The case touches on the long-running tension between the administration and major news outlets over coverage.

The order covers only the three organisations named in the suit. Press freedom advocates have welcomed the ruling, while the administration has defended the ban as a matter of internal White House operations.
