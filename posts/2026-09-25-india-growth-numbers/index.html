ANALYSIS | Let's talk numbers, not narratives.

1960: China's GDP $60 billion, India's $37 billion. Similar baselines. 2025: China $18 trillion, India $4.2 trillion.

From 1960 to 2014, India added about $1.66 trillion over 54 years. From 2014 to 2025, India added about $2.5 trillion in just 11 years, jumping from the 11th to the 4th largest economy in the world.

This is not about any political party. It is about every Indian's progress.

Celebrate what's right. Improve what's wrong. But never deny the progress.
