Apple is chasing another India record.

The company set an all-time India sales high of $10 billion last fiscal (FY26), and is now eyeing a new record this fiscal, after the iPhone 18 series saw stronger initial demand than its predecessors (IANS).

Analysts and retailers report initial demand for the 18 Pro and Pro Max up to 28% higher year-on-year. Counterpoint Research's Tarun Pathak says it is too early for firm figures, but 18 Pro demand is already outpacing the 17 Pro.

Apple skipped a standard iPhone 18 this year, concentrating demand on the two Pro models. CyberMedia Research's Prabhu Ram says structured retail financing is expanding Apple's revenue value and lifting average selling prices. The 18 Pro starts at Rs 1,64,900 and the Pro Max at Rs 1,79,900.
