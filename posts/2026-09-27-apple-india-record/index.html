Apple is chasing another India record.

Apple is chasing another India record. After posting an all-time India sales high of $10 billion last fiscal (FY26), the company is now eyeing a new record this fiscal, following stronger initial demand for the iPhone 18 series than its predecessors, according to IANS.

Analysts and retailers report that initial demand for the 18 Pro and Pro Max is up to 28 percent higher year-on-year. Tarun Pathak of Counterpoint Research cautions that it is too early for firm figures, but says demand for the 18 Pro is already outpacing the 17 Pro.

Betting big on the Pro models

Apple skipped a standard iPhone 18 this year, concentrating demand on the two Pro models. The 18 Pro starts at Rs 1,64,900 and the Pro Max at Rs 1,79,900, making this a premium-led push.

Prabhu Ram of CyberMedia Research says structured retail financing is expanding Apple's revenue value in India and lifting average selling prices, as more buyers opt for instalment plans on high-end devices.

If the momentum holds, Apple would extend a remarkable run in one of its fastest-growing major markets, where rising incomes and easy financing are steadily pulling premium buyers into its ecosystem.
