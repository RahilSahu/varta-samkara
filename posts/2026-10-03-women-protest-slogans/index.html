Delhi University's women students are protesting in rhyme, and the slogans are everywhere.

What is happening: - On Sept 21, a 17-year-old girl was allegedly gang-raped at Aastha Kunj Park in Kalkaji, Delhi. - Since then, women and queer students from Miranda House, Lady Shri Ram, Gargi and Kamala Nehru colleges have marched under the banner "Hum Auratein Azaad Hain" (We women are free). - 500+ women joined the South Campus march on Thursday, walking from Durgabai Deshmukh metro station to the campus gate. - This week the Supreme Court took suo motu cognisance of the recent cases, saying public spaces cannot become "zones of high risk" due to "lax patrolling", and sought responses from the Delhi government and police. - Their demands: safer public spaces, better lighting and transport, functional internal complaints committees, and no curfews on women in the name of safety.

A protest does not have to sound solemn to be serious.
