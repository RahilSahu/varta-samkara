OPINION | Reservation should continue, but it should reach those who need it most.

The case: reservation should primarily benefit EWS and first-generation beneficiaries from historically disadvantaged castes. Without a generational limit, families that already benefited can keep the advantage across generations, while families from the same caste who are still disadvantaged get left behind.

Help should be targeted, and it should stay competitive. The goal is equal opportunity, not permanent dependency.
