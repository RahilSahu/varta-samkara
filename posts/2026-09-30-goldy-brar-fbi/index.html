The FBI just put a $1 MILLION bounty on gangster Goldy Brar's head.

Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, was added to the FBI's Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list on September 28, becoming the 543rd fugitive on the historic list.

The reward for information leading to his arrest jumped from $50,000 to $1,000,000 (about Rs 9.58 crore).

The FBI says Brar is the alleged North American leader of the Lawrence Bishnoi Organised Crime Group, linked to assassinations, shootings, kidnappings, extortion and drug trafficking across the US and Canada. He faces RICO conspiracy, extortion and narcotics charges, with a federal arrest warrant from the US District Court in California.

FBI warning: consider him armed, dangerous and an escape risk.
