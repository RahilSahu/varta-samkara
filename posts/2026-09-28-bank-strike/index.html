3-Day Bank Strike Called Off: Banks Open as Usual

The planned 3-day nationwide bank strike (Sep 28-30) has been deferred. All public-sector bank branches will function normally, a major relief for customers.

The United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU), an umbrella body of seven bank employee and officer unions, called it off after a Sunday meeting with the Indian Banks Association (IBA).

The big concession: a high-level IBA-UFBU committee will be formed immediately to deliberate on a five-day workweek, i.e. declaring all Saturdays as holidays. Talks on changes to the Performance-Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for Scale IV and above officers will also begin.

SBI has issued a customer advisory confirming that all branches and banking services will continue without interruption.
