3-Day Bank Strike Called Off: Banks Open as Usual

The planned 3-day nationwide bank strike from September 28 to 30 has been deferred. All public-sector bank branches will function normally, in a major relief for customers who had braced for disruptions.

The United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU), an umbrella body of seven bank employee and officer unions, called off the strike after a Sunday meeting with the Indian Banks Association (IBA).

The big concession: a high-level IBA-UFBU committee will be formed immediately to deliberate on a five-day workweek, which would declare all Saturdays as holidays. Talks on changes to the Performance-Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for Scale IV and above officers will also begin.

SBI has issued a customer advisory confirming that all branches and banking services will continue without interruption. With the strike off, salary credits, cheque clearances and branch services will run as usual through the month end.
