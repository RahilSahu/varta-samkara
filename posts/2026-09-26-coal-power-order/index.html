India invokes emergency powers to run coal plants at full throttle

The power ministry has ordered 112 captive coal plants (50 MW and above) to run at maximum capacity from Oct 1 to Dec 31, 2026, invoking Section 11 of the Electricity Act.

Why: nearly 40% of coal-fired plants are running critically low on coal, and hotter-than-usual El Nino weather is pushing electricity demand higher. Plants owned by Tata Steel, Vedanta, Hindalco, JSW Steel, UltraTech Cement, Reliance, Indian Oil and Nayara Energy are covered. Surplus power must be sold on power exchanges, and plants must file weekly reports with the CEA. Separately, Tata Power's Mundra imported-coal plant was also told to run at full capacity till Dec 31.
