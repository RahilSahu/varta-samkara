SC says no to pausing UPI charges: 0.4% MDR on merchant payments above Rs 2,000 starts Oct 15

A 3-judge bench led by CJI Surya Kant refused on Monday to stay the Centre's 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate on specified UPI person-to-merchant transactions above Rs 2,000. The framework stays on track for October 15.

Person-to-person transfers stay completely free at any amount, and merchant payments up to Rs 2,000 stay free too. The 0.4% applies inside the merchant payment ecosystem, capped at Rs 300 for transactions of Rs 75,000 and above.

The Centre told the court the levy is "neither a tax nor a fee" and that "the Government of India is not taking a rupee of this". It says 96% of UPI users are exempt, and essentials like rail, telecom and fuel are capped at Rs 5.

The legal challenge continues: the PIL by advocate Anjan Datta led to notices to the Centre, RBI and NPCI, with replies due in four weeks.
