SC says no to pausing UPI charges: 0.4% MDR on merchant payments above Rs 2,000 starts Oct 15

A 3-judge bench led by CJI Surya Kant refused on Monday to stay the Centre's 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate on specified UPI person-to-merchant transactions above Rs 2,000. The framework stays on track for October 15.

Person-to-person transfers stay completely free at any amount, and merchant payments up to Rs 2,000 stay free too. The 0.4% applies inside the merchant payment ecosystem, capped at Rs 300 for transactions of Rs 75,000 and above.

The Centre told the court the levy is "neither a tax nor a fee" and that "the Government of India is not taking a rupee of this". It said 96% of UPI users are exempt, and essentials like rail, telecom and fuel are capped at Rs 5.

Legal challenge continues

The legal fight is not over. The PIL by advocate Anjan Datta has led to notices being issued to the Centre, the RBI and the NPCI, with replies due in four weeks. The court will take up the matter again after the responses come in.

For now, the October 15 rollout stands, and the exemption structure means the overwhelming majority of everyday UPI payments, from street vendors to small shops, remain untouched.
