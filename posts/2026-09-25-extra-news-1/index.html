INDIA | IAF to train with US F-35s for the first time at Tarang Shakti 26

The second edition of the IAF's multinational air exercise Tarang Shakti 2026 begins Sept 26 at Air Force Station Jodhpur, Rajasthan, running till Oct 12. Seven nations (US, France, Germany, Australia, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, UAE) bring air assets and around 32 attend as observers. US F-35 stealth fighters will join tactical drills with the IAF for the first time, confirmed by IAF Vice Chief Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit on Sept 22. The F-35 had visited India only for Aero India air shows before, never for joint combat training.
