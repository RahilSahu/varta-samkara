TRAI ends the forced 28-day recharge cycle

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India has issued the Telecom Consumer Protection (Thirteenth Amendment) Regulations, 2026. For every 30-day-or-less bundled plan, operators must now offer a matching voice-and-SMS-only plan at a lower tariff.

What changes for you: - True 30-day validity means 12 recharges a year instead of 13 - No more paying for data you never use - One plan that renews on the same date every month - Jio, Airtel and Vi all opposed the move, warning of fraud risks and bill shocks. TRAI went ahead anyway.

Plans roll out from 21 October 2026.
