TRAI ends the forced 28-day recharge cycle

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India has ended the forced 28-day recharge cycle, issuing the Telecom Consumer Protection (Thirteenth Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

Under the new rules, for every bundled plan with 30 days or less of validity, operators must now offer a matching voice-and-SMS-only plan at a lower tariff. The move is aimed at subscribers who are paying for data they never use.

What changes for you

True 30-day validity means 12 recharges a year instead of 13, cutting one full recharge cycle from the annual bill. Plans will renew on the same date every month, ending the slow calendar drift of the 28-day cycle.

The industry pushed back hard. Jio, Airtel and Vi all opposed the move, warning of fraud risks and bill shocks for consumers. TRAI went ahead anyway, siding with subscriber interests over operator concerns.

The new plans are set to roll out from October 21, 2026. For feature-phone users and anyone who rarely touches mobile data, the voice-and-SMS-only options could finally mean paying only for what is actually used.
