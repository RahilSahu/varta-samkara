ANALYSIS | The history of freebies in India.

2006: DMK in Tamil Nadu promises ₹300 monthly assistance and colour TVs. AIADMK follows. Then Akali Dal in Punjab, SP in UP (2012), AAP in Delhi (2015). Congress scales up: NYAY's ₹72,000/year (2019), Himachal's ₹1,500/month for women (2022), Karnataka and Telangana's guarantees (2023). TMC builds the Bengal model: Lakshmir Bhandar, Kanyashree, Rupashree, Aikyashree. In 2023, BJP joins with Ladli Behna in MP.

The BJP was not the pioneer of freebie politics. It was one of the last major parties to adopt it.

Now no government can touch freebies without risking the election. The argument: only the Supreme Court can end it, by making such promises illegal.
