LPU on boil: violent campus protests over alleged rape, classes suspended for 10 days.

Students at Lovely Professional University in Phagwara, Punjab, clashed with police on Sunday, September 27, over claims that a female student was assaulted on campus by an outsider. The university has suspended regular classes for 10 days starting Monday and postponed mid-term examinations until further notice.

The protest turned violent as demonstrators pelted stones, blocked NH-44 (Delhi-Amritsar) and set two police vehicles on fire. Several police personnel were injured in the clashes. Police responded with a lathi charge, and a large force led by the ADGP later entered the campus to bring the situation under control.

Police have registered an FIR against an unidentified person in connection with the alleged assault. An SIT has been formed and will examine CCTV footage, forensic evidence and call records as part of the investigation.

University denies allegation

The university has denied the allegation, calling it "baseless, fabricated rumours", and said it will cooperate fully with the probe. Campus authorities have asked students to stay calm and not believe unverified claims circulating on social media.

With classes suspended and mid-term exams postponed, thousands of students face uncertainty over their academic calendar. Police say the situation is being monitored and additional forces remain deployed in and around the campus.
