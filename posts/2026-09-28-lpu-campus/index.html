LPU on boil: violent campus protests over alleged rape, classes suspended for 10 days.

Students at Lovely Professional University in Phagwara, Punjab, clashed with police on Sunday, September 27, over claims that a female student was assaulted on campus by an outsider.

Two police vehicles were set on fire and several cops were injured as protesters pelted stones and blocked NH-44 (Delhi-Amritsar). Police responded with a lathi charge, and a large force led by the ADGP later entered the campus to bring the situation under control.

LPU has suspended regular classes for 10 days starting Monday and postponed mid-term exams until further notice. Police have registered an FIR against an unidentified person and formed an SIT that will examine CCTV, forensic and call records. The university denies the allegation, calling it "baseless, fabricated rumours", and says it will cooperate fully with the probe.
