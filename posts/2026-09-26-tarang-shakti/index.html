US F-35 stealth fighters join India's Tarang Shakti exercise for the first time

US F-35 stealth fighters are training on Indian soil for the first time, as the Indian Air Force's multinational exercise Tarang Shakti 2026 got underway at Air Force Station Jodhpur.

The exercise, which runs till October 12, brings together seven nations, the US, France, Germany, Australia, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and the UAE, flying their own aircraft alongside the IAF. Nearly 40 countries are taking part or observing.

A first for the F-35 in India

The participation of the American F-35, a fifth-generation stealth fighter, in a multinational combat exercise on Indian soil is a first. India is fielding a wide mix of its own fleet: the Rafale, Mirage-2000, Jaguar, Tejas, MiG-29 and Su-30 MKI.

IAF Vice Chief Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit underlined the significance of the moment: "For the first time, we are going to exercise with the F-35."

The exercise is designed to sharpen interoperability between friendly air forces, with complex combat scenarios flown together over Rajasthan's skies. It also marks a visible deepening of defence cooperation between India and the participating nations at a time when air power is being reshaped by stealth and networked warfare.
