No distress call. No flight data records. Two pilots dead.

The Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau's preliminary report into the August 27 Kanpur crash says Garg Aviation's Tecnam P2006T trainer made no distress call before going down near the Ganga Barrage, less than 20 minutes after takeoff.

A 39-year-old deputy chief flight instructor and a 28-year-old trainee pilot were killed. The aircraft was destroyed on impact.

The AAIB found the operator claimed it did "random monitoring" of the aircraft's Garmin flight data but kept no records and had no standard operating procedure for it. The bureau has asked the DGCA to issue specific flight-data monitoring guidelines for all flying training organisations.

A wider investigation

Both engines are quarantined for detailed examination, and Italy's ANSV and the US NTSB have joined the probe. The findings raise serious questions about oversight at flying training schools, and the DGCA's response to the AAIB's recommendation will set the tone for how training operations are supervised going forward.
