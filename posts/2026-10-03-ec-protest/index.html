700+ detained in Delhi. Thousands on the streets in Mumbai. The Election Commission is facing its biggest street test in years.

What happened on Oct 2:

DELHI - Opposition parties and student outfits (AAP, AISA, SFI, NSUI, DYFI, Indian Youth Congress) marched at Jantar Mantar demanding CEC Gyanesh Kumar's resignation. - Delhi Police detained 700+ protesters from Jantar Mantar and nearby areas. An earlier estimate was 150. - Detained: former Delhi CM Atishi, AAP Delhi chief Saurabh Bharadwaj, MLA Sanjeev Jha, RS MP Sanjay Singh, activist Yogendra Yadav, AISA president Neha Bora and NSUI president Vinod Jakhar. Around 700 SFI members were stopped at the Ashoka Road roundabout. - Police had denied permission a day earlier, with prohibitory orders under Section 163 BNSS in force in the New Delhi district. AAP says it applied six days in advance.

MUMBAI - On Gandhi Jayanti, the Cockroach Janta Party launched its 'Jail Bharo Andolan' at Shivaji Park. Thousands gathered despite permission being denied. - CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke was joined by Naseeruddin Shah, Shabana Azmi, Vishal Dadlani, Vishal Bhardwaj and Tushar Gandhi. Shiv Sena (UBT) and MNS extended support.

Why they are protesting: - Protesters allege irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and mass deletion of voters. The Indian Express reported that ECs Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi objected at least 14 times in 10 months over roll-related decisions. - The Election Commission rejects talk of a split and says all decisions were unanimous. The EC and the government deny manipulating rolls to favour the BJP.

What next: the CJP plans protests in six more cities, ending at Jantar Mantar by the end of October.
