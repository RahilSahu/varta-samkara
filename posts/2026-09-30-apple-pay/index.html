Apple Pay is finally live in India, and the iPhone just got its tap-to-pay moment.

Apple Pay went live in India on September 30, 2026, with Axis Bank as the first Indian bank to support it. India joins more than 90 countries where Apple Pay already works.

On day one, only Axis Bank credit cards on the Visa and Mastercard networks are supported. RuPay cards, debit cards and UPI payments are not supported at launch, and cards from HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and SBI Card are not included yet.

You can tap and pay in stores without entering a PIN or OTP, and check out similarly in apps and online, using an iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. Payments authenticate through Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, and Mac support is coming soon.

Where it works

It is accepted at Apple Store locations, Blinkit, Chaayos, Comet, Croma, Interflora, Ixigo, Le15 Patisserie, Reliance brands, Tata 1mg, Zomato and others, enabled across merchant networks by Cashfree, JusPay, Mswipe, Paytm, PayU, Pine Labs, Razorpay and Worldline. Apple says it does not retain transaction information that can be tied back to the user.

The launch is a limited first step, but it puts Apple Pay into one of the world's most competitive digital payments markets, where UPI still dominates.
