India ke 129 desi tech brands kamate hain Rs 60,000 CRORE. Lekin sirf 14 Rs 500 crore cross kar paaye. Kyun?

The Viksit Tech 47 Report 2026 (India Mobile Congress + Techarc), released Oct 8 at IMC 2026, tracks 129 homegrown brands in gadgets, devices, appliances and connected cars. Together they earn over Rs 60,000 crore a year, but the average is only ~Rs 465 crore per brand. Just two cross Rs 2,000 crore.

The report's TAME model explains why: brands move from Trade to Assemble to Manufacture to Engineer. Trading caps revenue near Rs 500 crore, assembly near Rs 5,000 crore, manufacturing near Rs 50,000 crore. "Revenues in lakhs of crores" come only at the Engineer stage, where brands own their design, software and IP.

"Most of these brands are still at the early stages of their evolution, trading or assembling products rather than engineering them," says Techarc co-founder Faisal Kawoosa.

The backdrop: India's electronics production hit Rs 13.11 lakh crore in FY26 (from Rs 1.90 lakh crore in FY15), exports reached Rs 4.24 lakh crore, but domestic value addition is only ~17%, and Indian brands hold a low single-digit share of the home smartphone market.

At 13% annual growth, the 129 brands could reach Rs 1.11 lakh crore by FY31 and Rs 2.04 lakh crore by FY36. The 2047 ambition: 50 Indian tech brands, each earning over Rs 1 lakh crore a year. Getting there needs ~25% growth every year for two decades.
