INDIA | India's Russian oil imports fell 16.5% in August

India's imports of Russian crude fell 16.5 percent month-on-month in August 2026 to about 2.1 million barrels per day, according to trade-source data reported by Reuters. Russia remains India's top oil supplier, followed by the UAE and Venezuela.

The decline was part of a broader pullback. India's total crude imports fell 8.8 percent to 4.44 million barrels per day in August. Imports from Iraq surged roughly 25 percent, while flows from the UAE and Saudi Arabia shifted, reshuffling the supplier mix.

Why the drop, and what comes next

Kpler data projects that Russian imports could fall further to around 1.9 million barrels per day in September, as refiners tap spot markets for October-November cargoes. The caution is driven by fears of US tariffs of up to 100 percent on buyers of Russian oil.

New Delhi has said it is "firmly committed" to energy security from diverse sellers, framing the shift as prudent sourcing rather than a political break.

It is worth keeping the nuance in view. Reuters attributes the drop mainly to Middle East supply shifts, including ADNOC logistics, tighter Russian availability and Chinese competition for the same barrels. The data does not, on its own, prove a deliberate refiner-led move away from Russia. September's numbers will show whether this is a blip or a trend.
