INDIA HAS A NEW ARCHERY HERO! Teenager Kumkum Mohod just made history in Nagoya.

What happened this morning:

- Kumkum Mohod won the women's individual recurve archery GOLD at the Asian Games 2026 on Saturday morning, October 3, in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. She is the first Indian EVER to win an individual recurve medal at the Asian Games, and she took the ultimate prize. - The final went to a shoot-off after the sets finished 5-5. Korea's Oh Yejin fired an 8; Kumkum answered with a 10 to seal the gold. She also shot a perfect 30/30 in set 3 of the final. - Her giant-killing run: in the quarterfinal she stunned reigning world champion Chaeyoung Kang of Korea 6-4, then beat China's Jingyi Zhu in the semifinal. - By reaching the final, Kumkum also locked India's women's individual recurve quota for the LA 2028 Olympics. - The gold is India's 17th of the 2026 Asian Games, lifting the medal tally to 77.
