THE $15 BILLION ESSAR BET ON AMERICAN STEEL

US President Donald Trump has unveiled plans by India's Essar Group to build a $15 billion steel complex in Iowa, calling it the largest steel plant in American history.

Announced at the White House on Sep 28 alongside Essar co-founder Ravi Ruia and his son Rewant Ruia, the project will be built by Mesabi Metallics, an Essar-owned company. Total US investment stands at $18 billion, including about $3 billion for the Minnesota mine and pellet plant.

The complex will produce 10 million tonnes of steel a year (about 7.5 million in the first phase), with production targeted to begin in 2030. It promises up to 1,750 permanent jobs in Iowa plus more than 8,000 construction jobs, and the White House estimates about $95 billion in economic output during construction and the first 10 years.

The plant will run on iron ore from Mesabi Metallics' new mine in Nashwauk, Minnesota, the first new iron ore mine built in the US in 50 years. It creates a fully integrated mine-to-mill chain using direct-reduced iron and electric arc furnaces, which cut energy use and emissions versus traditional blast furnaces.

Trump credited his 50% tariff on imported steel for pulling investment back into American steelmaking.
