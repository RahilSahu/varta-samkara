"We blow them up or make a deal." That is Donald Trump's ultimatum to Iran.

The US-Iran war took a sharp turn on October 1:

- US President Donald Trump told reporters in the Oval Office on Wednesday that the war with Iran will end "very soon, one way or the other." Washington, he said, must now choose between escalating and cutting a deal. - US Secretary of State Marco Rubio ordered Iran's delegation to the UN General Assembly, including Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, to leave the US immediately on Monday after indirect talks stalled. A US official said the delegation had "overstayed their welcome." - The White House had expected possible progress in the talks on Monday morning, but by late afternoon they had reached a stalemate. Araghchi and his team flew from New York to Doha at 1:20 am on Tuesday. Qatar is still mediating a compromise. - On Thursday the US also announced fresh sanctions on Iran's largest automakers, rail, manufacturing and metals companies, calling them the "critical arteries" of Iran's economy. - For India: the war began on February 28 and sits on the Strait of Hormuz, the energy route India imports most of its oil through. Any escalation is a fuel-price risk for India.
