INDIA | PB Fintech crashes 36% to 52-week low on IRDAI commission-cap plan

PB Fintech, the parent company of Policybazaar and Paisabazaar, suffered its steepest single-day fall since listing, closing 35.98 percent lower at Rs 1,210 on the BSE on September 24 and hitting a fresh 52-week low.

The trigger was a consultation paper released by the insurance regulator IRDAI on September 23, titled "Recalibrating Economics of Insurance Distribution." The paper proposes hard commission caps, nil on new-vehicle third-party cover, 15 percent on first sale and 5 percent on renewal for health insurance, along with tighter expense limits for insurers. Bernstein called the proposed cuts "much worse than imagined."

A sector-wide selloff

The pain was not limited to PB Fintech. Turtlemint, another insurance distribution platform, hit its 20 percent lower circuit on the same day, as investors priced in a sharp compression in distribution economics across the sector.

It is important to note what this paper is and is not. This is a consultation paper, not final law. IRDAI has invited comments till October 25, and the final framework could look very different from the draft.

Still, the market reaction shows how sensitive insurance distributors are to regulatory changes in commission structures, which form the core of their revenue. The coming weeks of industry feedback will be closely watched for signs of how far the regulator is willing to go.
