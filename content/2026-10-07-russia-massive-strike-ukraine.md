Russia launches massive overnight strike on Ukraine, killing 13 in Pryluky

Russia carried out what its defence ministry called a "massive strike" on Ukraine in the early hours of Wednesday, with the Ukrainian air force reporting 130 drones and 48 cruise missiles plus ballistic missiles fired across the country. Ukrainian air defences said they downed 161 targets. The death toll in the northern city of Pryluky rose to 13, including four children, with about 50 people injured, regional governor Vyacheslav Chaus said.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said energy infrastructure was among the key targets. "The Russians wanted to strike painfully and demonstratively," he wrote on Telegram. Explosions were heard in Kyiv around 3:15 am local time, and strikes also damaged buildings and warehouses in the capital and an industrial facility in Dnipro, Ukrainian officials said.

## Competing accounts

Russia's defence ministry said its forces struck military-industrial facilities in Kyiv and other regions, including sites where the Ukrainian firm Fire Point manufactures and stores Flamingo cruise missiles and medium and long-range drones. The attacks came hours after Zelenskyy warned in a video address on Tuesday night that intelligence showed Russia preparing a massive strike, and urged partners to supply air-defence weapons.

Meanwhile, RBC-Ukraine reported that Ukraine received 1.2 billion euros from the EU for defence needs on Wednesday, and quoted US President Donald Trump as saying the war in Ukraine was "actually nearing end."
