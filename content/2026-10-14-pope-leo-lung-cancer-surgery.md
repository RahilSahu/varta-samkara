Pope Leo XIV diagnosed with lung cancer after surgery, lower lobe of right lung removed

The Vatican said on Tuesday that Pope Leo XIV's surgery confirmed he has lung cancer, and that the lower lobe of his right lung was removed. The tests confirmed a diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma, according to a medical bulletin issued after the surgery.

## The diagnosis

The Vatican said the diagnosis had been suspected by preoperative tests and was confirmed during surgery. The bulletin described the procedure as the removal of the lower lobe of the right lung, following which the presence of lung adenocarcinoma was established.

## Recovery

The Vatican said the Pope is recovering after the surgery. No further details about the medical bulletin were immediately released beyond the confirmed diagnosis.
