Saudi coalition strikes Yemen oil sites after Houthi attack; blasts at Aramco facility

The Saudi-led coalition battling the Iran-backed Houthis struck oil sites across western Yemen on Tuesday, including in the capital Sanaa, in response to the deadliest attack on the Kingdom's soil in years. Blasts were heard shortly after in the Saudi capital Riyadh and at an oil giant Aramco facility in eastern Saudi Arabia, witnesses and journalists told AFP.

## Attack on Aramco

A building of Saudi Arabia's state oil company Aramco was attacked in Dhahran as the Houthi rebels in Yemen escalated their missile campaign against the kingdom, The National reported. The Energy Ministry said the strike caused a fire but there were no casualties.

## Coalition response

The Saudi-led coalition has urged civilians to evacuate the Yemeni Oil Company's Al-Sabah Central Station in Sanaa ahead of what it described as a planned "qualitative operation", according to reports.

## Advisory for Indian nationals

The Indian Embassy in Riyadh advised Indian nationals in Saudi Arabia to remain calm and vigilant and to stay indoors in case of an aerial attack, opening a 24x7 control room to assist the community.

Source: The Hindu, The National, AFP
