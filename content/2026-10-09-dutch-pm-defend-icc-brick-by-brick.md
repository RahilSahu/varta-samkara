Dutch PM Rob Jetten Vows to Defend ICC "Brick by Brick" Amid US Sanctions Push

Dutch Prime Minister Rob Jetten on Friday pledged to defend the International Criminal Court "brick by brick", a pointed response to Washington, where Secretary of State Marco Rubio has used the same phrase in threats to dismantle the tribunal.

Jetten told reporters at a regular briefing in The Hague that the Netherlands feels responsible for ensuring the ICC can function correctly and would "protect it brick by brick where necessary". The court, based in The Hague, faces unprecedented pressure from Washington, which has already sanctioned several top court officials and is considering sweeping penalties on the institution. President Trump is angered by the ICC's arrest warrant for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over alleged war crimes in Gaza, and the US also opposes its investigations of American soldiers over actions in Afghanistan.

Jetten said the Netherlands is working behind the scenes diplomatically to make clear to Washington that far-reaching sanctions would harm transatlantic ties, and noted several countries are working to propose reforms to the court. "And the message to the Americans is also: 'Join that conversation and prevent further escalation'," he said. He recalled his fiery address at the UN General Assembly last month, where he declared "Hands off the ICC". Under a 2008 headquarters agreement, the Netherlands holds special responsibility for the court's functioning.
