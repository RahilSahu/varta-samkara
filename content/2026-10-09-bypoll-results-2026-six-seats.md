Bypoll Results: BJP Sweeps West Bengal Seats, TVK Wins Both in Tamil Nadu, BJP Takes Assam Lok Sabha Seat

Votes were counted on Friday, October 9, for bypolls held on October 6 in five Assembly seats and one Lok Sabha constituency across West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Assam. The BJP won both West Bengal Assembly seats and the Nagaon Lok Sabha seat in Assam, actor-turned-politician Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) won both Tamil Nadu seats, and the ruling AINRC retained the Thattanchavady seat in Puducherry.

## West Bengal and Assam Go to BJP
In Nandigram, BJP candidate Hasirani Rath won by a margin of 1,22,753 votes, securing 1,64,427 votes. In Rejinagar, BJP candidate Shankharavh Sarkar emerged victorious with 1,29,914 votes. In Assam's Nagaon Lok Sabha seat, BJP candidate Devasish Sharma defeated AIUDF chief Badruddin Ajmal by 1,10,629 votes, with 4,20,705 votes to Ajmal's 3,10,076; Congress candidate Sibamoni Bora finished third with 75,908 votes. West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari, who held Nandigram before retaining Bhabanipur, said only, "We became friends."

## TVK Consolidates in Tamil Nadu; AINRC Holds Puducherry
TVK candidate Maragatham Kumaravel won Madurantakam by 19,241 votes, while P. Sathyabama won Dharapuram by 9,471 votes over DMK's Suganya. In Puducherry's Thattanchavady, AINRC candidate Ashok Anand defeated Congress rival E. Vinayagam by 8,482 votes (13,358 to 4,876), with DMK's K. Sethu Selvam third at 2,664 votes. The seat fell vacant after Chief Minister N. Rangasamy retained Mangalam. West Bengal Congress chief Subhankar Sarkar demanded re-polling, alleging genuine voters could not reach polling stations, a charge the BJP rejected. BJP leader Keya Ghosh claimed Rejinagar voters had rejected appeasement politics.
