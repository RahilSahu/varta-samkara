India Wins Four Gold and One Silver at 19th Astronomy Olympiad

The Indian team won four gold and one silver medal at the 19th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA), held in Hanoi, Vietnam, from September 25 to October 5, 2026. All five Indian participants returned with medals.

Pranjal Dixit from Hyderabad, Sumant Gupta from Delhi, Akshat Srivastava from Gurugram and Arnav Sehrawat from Faridabad won gold medals, while Harshil Amitkumar Patel from Vadodara bagged a silver.

## Joint third in medal tally

With four gold medals, India finished third jointly with host Vietnam in the medal tally, behind Iran and South Korea, which won five gold medals each. The competition was the largest IOAA so far, with 307 students from 64 countries taking part. In all, 51 gold, 48 silver and 60 bronze medals were awarded.

The Olympiad tested students across theory, which accounted for 50 per cent of the score, with observation and data analysis contributing 25 per cent each.

## Mentors and the year's tally

The Indian team was accompanied by Prof Bhaswati Mookerjea of the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai, and Prof Sanved Kolekar of the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru. Prof Aniket Sule of the Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (HBCSE-TIFR), which trains selected Indian students for the Olympiads, attended as the current president of IOAA.

According to HBCSE, since Indian participation in Astronomy Olympiads began in 1999, 49 per cent of students have won gold, 35 per cent silver and 13 per cent bronze. With this event, all senior-level Olympiads of the year, covering Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Astronomy, are concluded. Every Indian student won either a gold (16 students) or a silver medal (8 students) this year, the first time this has happened since participation in international Olympiads began in 1989.
