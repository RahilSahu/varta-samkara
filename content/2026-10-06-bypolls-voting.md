Voting Underway in Six Bypolls Across West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Assam; Counting on October 9

Polling began on Tuesday for byelections to five Assembly constituencies in West Bengal, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry, along with the Nagaon Lok Sabha constituency in Assam. Voting started at 7 am and was scheduled to continue until 6 pm in most constituencies, while polling in Assam's Nagaon seat ended at 5 pm. The counting of votes is scheduled for Friday, October 9, and the Election Commission said the electoral process is to be completed by October 11.

## The seats and why they fell vacant

Nandigram and Rejinagar in West Bengal, Madurantakam and Dharapuram in Tamil Nadu, and Thattanchavady in Puducherry are voting for Assembly bypolls, while Nagaon votes for the Lok Sabha seat vacated by the resignation of Pradyut Bordoloi. In West Bengal, Nandigram fell vacant after Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari chose to retain Bhabanipur, where he defeated former CM Mamata Banerjee in the recent Assembly elections; the BJP has fielded Hasirani Rath there. Rejinagar fell vacant after Aam Janata Unnayan Party chief Humayun Kabir resigned as MLA. In Tamil Nadu, Madurantakam and Dharapuram fell vacant following the resignations of Maragatham Kumaravel and P Sathyabama. Puducherry's Thattanchavady seat was vacated by CM N Rangasamy, who retained Mangalam.

## Polling arrangements

The Election Commission deployed EVMs and VVPATs at all polling stations, with adequate security, webcasting at polling stations in the West Bengal constituencies, and elaborate arrangements in Puducherry's Thattanchavady, where 11 candidates are in the fray in what is seen as a three-cornered contest between the AINRC-led NDA, the Congress and the DMK. Early turnout reporting by the Indian Express put Madurantakam at 83.86 percent and Nandigram at 79.39 percent as of 5 pm.
