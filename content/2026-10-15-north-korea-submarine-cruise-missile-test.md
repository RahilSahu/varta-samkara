North Korea test-fires submarine-launched cruise missiles, KCNA says

North Korea's Missile Administration test-fired two kinds of submarine-launched strategic cruise missiles in the country's eastern waters on Wednesday, the official Korean Central News Agency (KCNA) said on Thursday.

## What is known

The KCNA report described two kinds of strategic cruise missiles launched from under the sea in the country's eastern waters. No further technical details were given in the reports, and independent confirmation of the test was not immediately available.

Source: Xinhua, Open Chronicle
