Cabinet approves sale of IDBI Bank to Fairfax for Rs 52,878 crore, the largest FDI in India's financial sector

The Union Cabinet on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, approved the strategic restructuring of IDBI Bank, with Canadian investor Fairfax set to acquire a stake in the lender for Rs 52,878 crore, the government announced. The deal is the largest-ever foreign direct investment in India's financial sector.

## Deal structure

Under the approved restructuring, the government's stake in IDBI Bank will fall to 15%, while Life Insurance Corporation's (LIC) stake will come down to 19%. The announcement marks a major step in the long-running disinvestment and strategic sale process for the bank.

## Government position

The government described the decision as a strategic restructuring of the lender. The Cabinet approval clears the way for Fairfax's entry as a major shareholder in the public-sector lender.
