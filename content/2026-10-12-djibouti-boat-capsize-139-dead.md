At Least 139 Dead and 84 Missing After Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Djibouti

At least 139 migrants, including 15 children, have died and 84 remain missing after a boat carrying 305 people capsized off Djibouti on its way to Yemen, the International Organization for Migration said on Sunday.

## What the agency reported

The vessel went down near Guhere, north of the port town of Obock, during the night of October 8 to 9, shortly after departure, with overcrowding cited by survivors. About 82 people survived, all Ethiopian nationals, the agency said. The toll rose from 109 after 30 more bodies were recovered, and search and rescue operations by Djiboutian authorities were continuing.

## Route and response

IOM teams are providing medical care, psychosocial support, food and shelter at the Migrant Response Centre in Obock. The agency said tens of thousands of people travel each year from the Horn of Africa through Djibouti across the Bab el-Mandeb Strait towards Yemen and Gulf countries, and that more than 900 people died or went missing on the route in 2025. IOM Director General Amy Pope called for expanded safe and legal pathways.
