Tamil Composer Deva, Who Took Chennai's Gaana to Mainstream Cinema, Dies at 75

Tamil film music composer Deva, who brought gaana music into mainstream cinema, has died aged 75.

## A bridge between worlds

Deva's songs and background scores for Rajinikanth-starrers such as Annamalai, Arunachalam, and Baashha became hugely popular across Tamil Nadu. Hospital sources said he had no pulse when he arrived, and efforts to revive him remained futile.

His music bridged seemingly disparate worlds: the earthy rhythms of Chennai's gaana tradition, the mass appeal of Tamil cinema, and the melodic richness of classical and devotional music.

## Legacy

Fondly known in the industry as a music director who democratised film music, Deva composed hundreds of songs and mentored several musicians. Tributes poured in from the Tamil film industry on Saturday.
