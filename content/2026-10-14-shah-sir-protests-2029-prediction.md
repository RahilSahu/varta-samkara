Shah slams SIR protests, predicts fourth consecutive Congress defeat in 2029

Union Home Minister Amit Shah on Tuesday said the Congress will suffer its fourth consecutive defeat in the 2029 Lok Sabha elections, while the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) will return to power with an overwhelming majority. He made the remarks while criticising the ongoing protests against the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls.

## The Opposition's position

Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi hosted Opposition politicians, civil society activists, and student leaders for tea on Tuesday to discuss the future course of the agitation against the SIR and the demand for the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Gandhi said: "There are no parallel protests, but only one movement to restore democracy."

Actor Prakash Raj criticised the arrest and five-day judicial custody of Cockroach Janata Party (CJP) leaders Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka after they entered Jantar Mantar with the national flag, calling the action a "big joke". He questioned the Centre's handling of the issue and demanded that the Special Intensive Revision be scrapped.

## Supreme Court's role

The Supreme Court on Wednesday agreed to intervene on behalf of Delhi's voters whose names were deleted from the electoral roll pursuant to the SIR, after the Election Commission effectively asked them to apply afresh through Form 6 to restore their names.
