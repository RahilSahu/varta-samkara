Titan reports 25% growth in consumer business sales for Q2, shares slip as investors turn cautious

Tata group company Titan on Tuesday reported a 25 per cent year-on-year rise in sales in its consumer businesses for the July-September quarter, driven by demand for jewellery, watches and eyewear. Despite the strong update, the stock slipped over four per cent in early trade on Wednesday as investors weighed the numbers ahead of the RBI's policy decision.

Titan added 78 net stores during the quarter, taking its combined retail network to 3,758 stores as of September 2026. Domestic businesses grew 22 per cent year-on-year, while the international business surged 97 per cent.

## Jewellery, watches and EyeCare all grow over 20%

The jewellery business, Titan's largest, grew around 21 per cent. The company said consumer demand stayed healthy for most of the quarter before softening towards the end as the festive calendar shifted into the third quarter. Studded jewellery grew in the early thirties, supported by the 'Festival of Diamonds' campaign, while plain gold jewellery grew around 20 per cent. Buyer growth was in the mid-single digits while average ticket sizes rose in double digits, pointing to continued premiumisation. Tanishq, Mia, Zoya and beYon together grew 20 per cent, while CaratLane stood out at 32 per cent.

The watches business recorded around 30 per cent growth, with analog watches up in the early thirties and smartwatches recovering to high single-digit growth. EyeCare posted 28 per cent growth from a year earlier.

Analysts retained positive ratings after the update. Citi maintained its 'Buy' rating with a share price target of Rs 5,700, Morgan Stanley kept 'Overweight' with a target of Rs 5,483, while JPMorgan kept 'Overweight' but lowered its target to Rs 5,540 from Rs 5,650.

The results add to signs of resilient consumer demand, with Trent and Marico also reporting strong second-quarter updates earlier this week.
