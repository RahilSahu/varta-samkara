IndiGo Raises Fuel Surcharge on Domestic and International Flights

IndiGo, the country's largest airline, on Monday announced a hike in fuel surcharge for domestic and international flights, citing a sharp rise in aviation turbine fuel (ATF) prices. The revised charges apply to all new bookings made from October 6, 2026.

The airline said ATF prices have risen steadily in recent months, with the latest month-on-month increase exceeding 14 per cent, pushing fuel costs to among their highest levels in the last decade. IndiGo noted that fuel accounts for a significant share of an airline's operating costs and that the increase is expected to affect cost structures and network economics across the sector.

## Revised surcharge slabs

On domestic routes, the surcharge will be Rs 375 for distances up to 500 km, Rs 600 for flights between 501 and 1,000 km, Rs 900 for distances between 1,001 and 1,500 km, Rs 1,150 for flights between 1,501 and 2,000 km, and Rs 1,300 for routes exceeding 2,000 km. The quantum of the hike ranges from Rs 100 to Rs 350 depending on distance.

For international travel, flights within the SAARC region will carry a surcharge of Rs 1,000 for distances up to 500 km and Rs 3,000 beyond that. Flights to Southeast Asia, the GCC, the Middle East and North and East Asia will attract Rs 5,500, while services to Africa and Europe will carry Rs 6,000.

## Airline's position

This is the second time since April that the carrier has increased the fuel surcharge, which was first introduced in March. The airline said it had opted for a measured increase despite the sharp rise in fuel expenses, noting that fully offsetting the higher ATF costs would have required a substantially larger adjustment.

IndiGo attributed the increase to persistent volatility in global crude oil prices, with uncertainty over geopolitical tensions keeping fuel costs elevated.
