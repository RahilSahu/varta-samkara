Rajnath Singh Felicitates Services Athletes for Record 27 Medals at Asian Games
Defence Minister Rajnath Singh on Monday felicitated Services sportspersons in New Delhi for winning 27 medals at the Asian Games in Japan, comprising 6 gold, 11 silver and 10 bronze, the best ever performance by the Services at the event.
## Contribution to India tally
A total of 92 athletes from the three Services competed in 19 disciplines. India finished fourth overall with 85 medals, comprising 21 gold, 27 silver and 37 bronze. Services athletes accounted for 32 percent of India medals and 28.57 percent of its gold medals, the Defence Ministry said.
## Standout performers
The ministry listed Naib Subedar Neeru Dhanda, who won two gold and one silver and became the first Indian woman to win individual women trap gold at the Games, and Naib Subedar Gulveer Singh, who won two silver and one bronze. Havildar Sawan Barwal won marathon silver in a national record 2:11:37, ending a 44 year wait for an Indian Asian Games marathon medal. The minister credited the Mission Olympic Wing and support staff and called for focused preparation for the Los Angeles Olympics in 2028.
