150 Bangladeshi Nationals Staying Illegally in J-K Being Deported

Jammu and Kashmir authorities on Monday said 150 Bangladeshi nationals who were staying illegally in the Union Territory were being deported. The group was flown on a special Indian Air Force plane to Bagdogra in West Bengal, where they were handed over to Border Security Force (BSF) personnel for onward repatriation to Bangladesh.

Officials said another batch of 150 Bangladeshi nationals would be deported on October 7.

## How they were identified

The deportations follow a survey conducted by the Jammu and Kashmir administration with the help of the Jammu and Kashmir Police across the Kashmir Valley, during which around 600 Bangladeshi nationals were found allegedly staying illegally in the region. The identified individuals were kept at a camp manned by the Srinagar Police before being transported to Bagdogra.

One official said the individuals had been working as labourers in Kashmir, and that discrepancies surfaced when they were asked to produce Aadhaar cards during a registration exercise. Their details were shared with authorities in Bangladesh, which confirmed their nationality, after which action was initiated under the provisions of the Foreigners Act.

## Wider exercise on illegal immigration

The action aligns with a broader exercise by the Union Home Ministry on demographic changes linked to illegal immigration. The Ministry constituted a High-Level Committee on Demographic Change (HLCDC) on May 26 to study such changes arising from illegal immigration and recommend measures to address them, with one year to submit its report. The J&K administration had informed the committee about the identification of around 600 Bangladeshi nationals across various districts of the Union Territory.
