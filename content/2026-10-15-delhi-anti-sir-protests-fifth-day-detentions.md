Anti-SIR protests in Delhi enter fifth day as hundreds are detained

Protests against the special intensive revision (SIR) of electoral rolls entered their fifth consecutive day in Delhi on Wednesday, with hundreds of people taking to the streets of central Delhi. Protesters climbed over barricades and detention buses filled up as police moved in against demonstrators also demanding the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

## Allegations of police excesses

People detained during the Delhi protests on October 10, 11 and 12 have filed petitions in the Supreme Court alleging police excesses. They have appealed for an independent inquiry into the police action, preservation of CCTV footage and bodycam video, and the return of their phones and belongings, alleging they were dragged, slapped and assaulted by Delhi Police personnel.
