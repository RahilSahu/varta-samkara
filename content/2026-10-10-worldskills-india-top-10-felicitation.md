India breaks into WorldSkills top 10 for first time, ministry felicitates medal-winning team

New Delhi: The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship on Friday felicitated Team India's competitors for their historic performance at the WorldSkills Competition Shanghai 2026, where the country secured its first-ever position among the world's top 10, finishing 10th globally with six silver medals and 20 Medals for Excellence.

## Best-ever result at the global skills contest

The result improves on India's 13th-place finish at the 2024 WorldSkills event in Lyon, where the country won four bronze medals and 12 Medals for Excellence. According to officials, the six silver medals at Shanghai were won by Smriti Nambiar in Bakery, Mohammed Salam in Dental Prosthetics, Kovid Ajay Gangrade in Digital Interactive Media Design, Parth Vohra in Industrial Design Technology, Mehrunissa Begum in Logistics and Freight Forwarding, and Saniya Joshi in Retail Sales. Smriti Nambiar was additionally recognised as the Best of the Nation for India.

## Felicitation and political response

Prime Minister Narendra Modi met the competitors at 7, Lok Kalyan Marg in New Delhi on Friday, where they discussed adapting to new tools, software, technical systems and work processes encountered during the competition. JSPL Chairman Naveen Jindal, who was in Shanghai during the event, said the government support for skills training had lifted India from 13th to 10th and predicted an even better showing next time.

The biennial event, held from September 22 to 27, brought together more than 1,400 young competitors from around 70 countries and regions. India fielded its largest-ever contingent of 70 competitors across 63 skills.
