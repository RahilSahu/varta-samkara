CBI Arrests CGST Superintendent in Bhopal in Rs 2 Lakh Monthly Bribe Case

The Central Bureau of Investigation has arrested a superintendent of the Central Goods and Services Tax in Bhopal for allegedly demanding a monthly bribe of Rs 2 lakh from a scrap trader, officials said on Sunday, October 11.

Rajesh Kumar, posted in the Preventive Branch (Headquarters) of the CGST in Bhopal, was arrested on Saturday in a trap operation, the agency said. According to the CBI, the case was registered on Saturday after a complaint that he had demanded Rs 2 lakh per month from the proprietor of a Bhopal based firm dealing in scrap material, in exchange for not intercepting vehicles carrying scrap and not obstructing the business.

## Cash recovered in searches

The CBI said it laid a trap and caught the accused while he was allegedly accepting Rs 2 lakh. Searches at his residential premises in Bhopal and Kanpur led to recovery of Rs 72.5 lakh in cash and documents related to immovable properties, the agency said in a statement.

The investigation is ongoing. The accused has not been convicted, and the allegations are those stated by the investigating agency based on the complaint and the trap proceedings.
