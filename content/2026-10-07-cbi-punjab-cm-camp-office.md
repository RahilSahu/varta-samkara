CBI Books Punjab CM's OSD for Graft, Searches Camp Office After High Court Order

The Central Bureau of Investigation on Tuesday registered a corruption case against Rajbir Singh Ghuman, Officer on Special Duty to Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann, and conducted coordinated searches at eight locations, including Mann's camp office in Chandigarh.

The CBI said it acted on the orders of the Punjab and Haryana High Court, which on October 5 directed the agency to register an FIR and investigate based on a report from the Enforcement Directorate (ED). The court asked the agency to submit a status report within two months.

According to the CBI statement, the FIR concerns allegations that Ghuman and his associates influenced and manipulated postings of officers, including IAS and IPS officers, departmental proceedings, policy formulation, administrative approvals, tender conditions, licensing, land use conversion, property disputes and revenue matters in return for pecuniary benefits. The agency said it is alleged the accused also accessed drafts of government orders and policies and shared them with private persons before official issuance. Six persons have been booked, including two public servants. Laptops, mobile phones and documents seized during the searches were sent for forensic examination.

Chief Minister Mann called the action political, saying the BJP was resorting to central agencies as Punjab elections approach. AAP national convener Arvind Kejriwal described the raid as the result of the BJP's frustration. The BJP's position, reported by legal outlets, is that the investigation was ordered by a court and not by the party. The Punjab government has challenged the High Court order in the Supreme Court.
