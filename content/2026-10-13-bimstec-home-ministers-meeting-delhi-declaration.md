First BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting Concludes in New Delhi with Delhi Declaration and Five Security Initiatives

India hosted the first BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting in New Delhi on Tuesday, chaired by Union Home Minister Amit Shah, with delegations from all seven member states. The meeting concluded with the adoption of the Delhi Declaration and approval of five regional security initiatives.

## Five initiatives approved

The approved initiatives are: the BIMSTEC Convention against trafficking in persons; a BIMSTEC plan of action on drug control; a memorandum of understanding on capacity building to combat money laundering and terror financing; a plan of action on illicit financial flows and their prevention; and a plan of action to improve preparedness and coordination for natural disasters in the Bay of Bengal region.

## Shah's remarks

Addressing the meeting, Shah reaffirmed India's zero-tolerance policy against terrorism and said India's security cooperation with neighbours should leave no room for harbouring, recruiting, training or raising resources for terrorist activity in the region. He said criminal networks operate across borders and take advantage of local weaknesses, and that regional cooperation therefore holds a special place in the fight against terrorism, organised crime and drug trafficking.

## About the grouping

The Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC) comprises seven member states: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand. India had announced on October 12 that it would host the inaugural meeting of the grouping's Home Ministers.
