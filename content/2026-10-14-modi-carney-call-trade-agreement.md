Modi speaks to Carney, says India and Canada contribute to a stable and prosperous world

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke to Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney on Tuesday, after which Modi said India and Canada are together contributing to a "more stable and prosperous world". The two leaders agreed to conclude a trade agreement soon.

## Trade and partnership

Both leaders agreed to build on the positive momentum in bilateral ties and work towards concrete outcomes across key areas of the strategic partnership. These areas include trade, energy, technology, security, and people-to-people ties, according to reports.

## A warm conversation

The two leaders had a warm and productive telephonic conversation, with both sides signalling intent to move the economic and strategic relationship forward in the coming months.
