Nana Patekar Dies at 75; Cremated With Full State Honours at Pune Farmhouse

Veteran actor Nana Patekar, known for acclaimed performances in Hindi and Marathi cinema, died in the early hours of Thursday, October 8, 2026, at his Goa residence at the age of 75. Doctors said he was suspected to have suffered a heart attack.

## Full State Honours at Donje
Thousands bid farewell late on Thursday night as his mortal remains were consigned to flames at his farmhouse in Donje village in Pune district. The last rites were performed with full state honours, including a three-gun salute, amid chants of "Nana Amar Rahe". His son, Malhar Patekar, lit the pyre. Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis paid tribute at Pune airport when the remains arrived from Goa; Deputy Chief Ministers Eknath Shinde and Sunetra Pawar, Union Ministers Ramdas Athawale and Murlidhar Mohol, and film figures Jackie Shroff, Anil Sharma, Sajid Nadiadwala and Anees Bazmee attended.

## Police Inquiry Opened
Panaji Police have registered an unnatural death case under Section 194 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, ANI reported on Friday. Manipal Hospital, Bambolim, had informed police around 8.07 am on October 8 that a 75-year-old man was brought dead from his Ocean Park residence in Dona Paula. The inquiry is being handled by Police Sub-Inspector Nitin Naik. A post-mortem was conducted at Goa Medical College. Patekar, remembered for films such as Krantiveer and Khamoshi: The Musical, also founded the Naam Foundation for drought-hit farmers. He is survived by his wife and son.
