India Draft Steel Policy Targets 604 Million Tonnes Capacity by 2047
India has released a draft National Steel Policy, Vision 2047, for public consultation, proposing crude steel capacity of 604 million tonnes by 2047, up from about 220 million tonnes at present.
## Targets in the draft
The draft projects finished steel demand of 505 million tonnes by 2047, up from 164 million tonnes in 2025-26, and per capita consumption rising to 302 kg from 116 kg. Exports are projected at 45 million tonnes. It would replace the National Steel Policy 2017 and projects capacity of 294 million tonnes by 2030.
## Raw materials and emissions
Raw materials account for 65 to 70 percent of steelmaking costs, the draft notes. India imports about 92 percent of its coking coal needs. Coking coal demand is projected to rise from 72 million tonnes to 236 million tonnes by 2047, and iron ore demand from 299 million tonnes to 772 million tonnes. The draft proposes overseas assets, expanded washeries and better coal blending. It aims to cut average emission intensity from 2.54 tonnes of carbon dioxide per tonne of crude steel to about 1.54 tonnes by 2047, in line with the 2070 net zero goal.
