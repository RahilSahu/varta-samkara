DMK Demands Removal of CEC Gyanesh Kumar, Return to Paper Ballots

A meeting of DMK MPs has demanded the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, alleging that he oversaw the deletion of voters' names under the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls.

## The resolution

The resolution, adopted at a meeting chaired by DMK president M.K. Stalin, said: "He must be removed and subjected to an appropriate inquiry." The party alleged that the CEC had overridden the objections of the other two Election Commissioners in pushing through the deletions.

The DMK also demanded a return to paper ballots, reviving a long-standing opposition demand for verifiable voting.

## Context

The demand comes amid sustained protests in Delhi led by the Citizen Rights Protection (CJP) movement over the SIR exercise and the conduct of the Election Commission. The Commission has not yet publicly responded to the DMK resolution.
