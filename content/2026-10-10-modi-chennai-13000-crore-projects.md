PM Modi to Launch Projects Worth Over Rs 13,000 Crore in Chennai on October 11

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Chennai on Sunday, October 11, to inaugurate and lay foundation stones for development projects worth more than Rs 13,000 crore, the Prime Minister's Office announced on Saturday. The projects cover Metro rail, national highways, railways, ports, petroleum infrastructure and education, and span multiple districts of Tamil Nadu.

The centrepiece of the visit is the inauguration of the Poonamallee-Vadapalani stretch of Chennai Metro Rail Phase II. Built at a cost of around Rs 4,900 crore, the 14.6-km corridor features a 3.7-km double-decker structure and signalling systems designed to support driverless operations. The Prime Minister is scheduled to ride the Metro from Porur Bypass to Poonamallee Bypass and back at 11.30 am.

## Highway, rail and port projects

The Prime Minister will inaugurate National Highway projects worth around Rs 3,700 crore, including the 36-km, four-lane Vadugapatti-Therkuvenganallur section of NH-744 and the 30-km Madurai Ring Road section of NH-744A. He will also lay the foundation stone for the four-lane Thiruvarur Bypass on NH-83.

Railway projects worth over Rs 750 crore include the Karaikal-Peralam line, three rail flyovers in and around Coimbatore, and five redeveloped Amrit Stations at Palani, Vellore Cantonment, Ambasamudram, Paramakkudi and Namakkal.

Port projects include upgraded storage, warehouses, cruise passenger facilities and a Seafarers Club at Chennai Port; eight projects at V.O. Chidambaranar Port supporting cargo capacity, cleaner maritime energy and logistics; and the inauguration of Coal Berth-3 at Kamarajar Port along with dredging works. The Prime Minister will also lay the foundation stone for CPCL's Rs 1,620-crore lubricant base stocks project at the Manali refinery.

## Temple and IIT Madras on itinerary

PM Modi is scheduled to visit the Jalakandeswarar Temple at 12.15 pm, followed by a walkthrough of the Brain Centre at IIT Madras. He will also attend the golden jubilee celebrations of Jaigopal Garodia Hindu Vidyalaya Matriculation Higher Secondary School.

Chennai has been placed under a multi-tier security blanket with around 15,000 police personnel deployed, as Congress leader Rahul Gandhi was also in the city on Saturday for a students' interaction and a meeting with Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay.
