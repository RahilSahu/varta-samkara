Bengaluru to Host FIBA Women's Basketball League Asia from October 20

Bengaluru will host the FIBA Women's Basketball League Asia 2026 from October 20 to 25 at the Sree Kanteerava Indoor Stadium, Deputy Chief Minister G. Parameshwara announced on Monday, marking the first time an Indian city hosts a FIBA Asia-level club competition.

## Six clubs from across Asia

The championship will feature the domestic league champions of China, Japan, South Korea and Chinese Taipei, alongside India's national champions and a wild card entry from the Philippines. The teams are Sichuan Yuanda Meile of China, Denso Iris of Japan, Cheongju KB Stars of South Korea, Cathay Life Tigers of Chinese Taipei, Indian Railways, and the Akari Sparks of the Philippines. Sichuan Yuanda Meile won the inaugural edition in 2024 and Dongguan New Century lifted the 2025 title, both in China, making Bengaluru the first host outside the country; Cathay Life Tigers will be the only team appearing for a third consecutive year.

## The event plan

The tournament is being organised by the Karnataka State Basketball Association under the aegis of the Basketball Federation of India, with support from the Department of Youth Empowerment and Sports and FIBA. All matches will be played at the Sree Kanteerava Indoor Stadium, with two games scheduled daily at 4 pm and 7 pm. Parameshwara said each team would field two American players and that NBA players would also take part, adding to the quality of the competition.

## What it means for Indian basketball

Parameshwara said the Karnataka government is committed to developing Bengaluru into a national and international basketball hub and would extend all necessary support to the event. He said the championship would let young players in Karnataka and across India watch leading international players and learn from their skills, techniques and competitive approach. He added that the event would help promote women's basketball and inspire more young girls to take up the sport. On Indian Railways' participation, he said the national champions would get a rare opportunity to compete against some of Asia's strongest clubs and gain international exposure.
