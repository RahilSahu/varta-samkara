PM Modi Calls for Global Framework Against Cyber Fraud and Deepfakes at India Mobile Congress

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Thursday called for a global framework to tackle cross-border cyber fraud and the growing threat of deepfakes, urging governments, technology companies, telecom operators and financial institutions to come together to stop such frauds before they reach people. Inaugurating the 10th edition of the India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026 in New Delhi, he said India had gained considerable experience in tackling cyber fraud and was ready to share its learnings with the world.

Modi said technology achieves scale only when it gains trust, and warned that cyber frauds and cyber crimes do not respect borders, with a person sitting in one country able to use another to commit a crime in a third. He specifically urged the GSMA and telecom operators across the world to help create a global mechanism that stops fraud at the source. At the start of his address, he also condoled the death of veteran actor Nana Patekar.

## 5G reaches every district, 5.5 lakh base stations

Recalling that he launched 5G from the same stage in 2022, the Prime Minister announced that 5G networks now run on over 5.5 lakh base stations and have reached every district, boosting internet speeds tenfold. He said the mobile phone had become a central tool of governance and public-service delivery under the Digital India vision, and that the digital divide of a decade ago had been bridged. This year's IMC theme is "Scale Without Boundaries".
