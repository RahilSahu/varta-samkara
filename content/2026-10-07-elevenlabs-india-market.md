ElevenLabs Names India Its Second-Largest Market, Plans Hundreds of Millions in Investment

AI voice startup ElevenLabs has named India its second-largest market after the United States and said it will invest hundreds of millions of dollars in the country, chief executive Mati Staniszewski announced at the company's first India Summit in Bengaluru on October 6.

The investment will go toward local teams, Indian-language models, and possible acquisitions, the company said. ElevenLabs' India page lists 12 Indian languages and accents, and Indian companies including Meesho and Cars24 already use its voice technology.

## Why India matters to the company

India's large base of voice-first internet users and its linguistic diversity make it a natural growth market for AI audio products. The planned spending signals that global AI firms are moving beyond serving India remotely and are building dedicated local operations, teams, and language models.

The announcement comes as competition in AI voice and audio intensifies in India, with both global players and domestic startups chasing developers building voice products in Hindi, Tamil, and other Indian languages.
