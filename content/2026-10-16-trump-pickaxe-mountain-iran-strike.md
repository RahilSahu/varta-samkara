Trump Says US May Have to Strike Iran's Pickaxe Mountain, Rules Out Nuclear Weapons

US President Donald Trump said on Thursday that the United States may have to target Pickaxe Mountain, a site linked to Iran's nuclear programme, while ruling out the use of nuclear weapons and US ground troops in the war that began in February with US-Israeli strikes.

## What Trump told Newsmax

In an interview with Newsmax on Thursday night, Trump said Washington wanted to ensure Iran would never have a nuclear weapon. "Their nuclear is buried under millions of tons of mountain. Now we're looking at Pickaxe Mountain. We may have to go there. We'll take a shot at that one. We'll knock it out fast," he told the news outlet. He added, "I wouldn't use nuclear. We don't have to," and reiterated he was not considering sending US ground troops into Iran.

## The site and the MOAB question

Pickaxe Mountain is a fortified facility buried deep underground near one of Tehran's main nuclear sites. Trump did not say whether he had authorised the use of the GBU-43/B Massive Ordnance Air Blast Bomb, known as the "mother of all bombs," against Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites, but said the weapon was not a nuclear bomb. CNN has reported that the US government is considering using the massive conventional bomb against Iran.
