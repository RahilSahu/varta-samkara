Russia fires 39 ballistic missiles and over 120 drones at Ukraine; Kyiv slams Starlink throttling

Russia attacked Ukraine with 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 drones and loitering munitions overnight, targeting energy and critical infrastructure in Kyiv, the Kyiv region, and Kharkiv, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said on October 14. The attacks left some 600,000 people without power.

## Targeted strike on energy infrastructure

Zelensky said a targeted Russian ballistic missile strike specifically hit energy infrastructure. The attack came for the second night in a row, and Ukraine has said it has also faced gaps in early warning about incoming ballistic missile attacks from its partners.

## Starlink row

Ukrainian officials said on Wednesday that Elon Musk's SpaceX had limited Kyiv's access to its Starlink satellite service without warning, disrupting combat drone operations. The throttling capped the velocity of Ukrainian drones at 100 miles per hour, a source told the New York Post, making high-speed UAV, aircraft, and helicopter operations impossible and affecting efforts to counter Russian Shahed drones.

Presidential adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov said Ukraine's defence forces received no warning of the limitation, which disrupted several missions. Ukraine's military relies on Starlink, for which Poland pays some 50 million dollars per year, and Kyiv's contract runs through May 15, 2027.

Musk later posted on X that "Zelensky has gone too far" and added "I agree, time for Ukraine to get a new leader". Polish Foreign Minister Radoslaw Sikorski responded: "Hey, @elonmusk, big man, are you playing politics with your products again? As your European customers, we are watching you."

## Zelensky's appeal

Zelensky urged the United States and other countries to respond forcefully, saying Russia had squandered the opportunity for de-escalation and chosen to continue the war. President Trump has also announced, after a call with Russian President Vladimir Putin, a deal to import hundreds of thousands of tons of Russian diesel.
