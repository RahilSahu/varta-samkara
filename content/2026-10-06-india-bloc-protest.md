INDIA Bloc March to Election Commission HQ in Delhi; Leaders Detained, Gandhi Siblings Stage Dharna

Opposition MPs of the INDIA bloc on Tuesday marched from the Parliament complex toward the Election Commission of India headquarters in Delhi, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. Delhi Police stopped the march on Parliament Street, where several leaders sat on dharna, and briefly detained opposition leaders including Rahul Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav, Trinamool Congress MP Abhishek Banerjee and Shiv Sena (UBT) MP Sanjay Raut.

## Standoff on Parliament Street

The march, held during the Parliament session, was halted by layers of police barricades about half a kilometre from Parliament near the Niti Aayog office on Parliament Street. Congress MP Jothimani said the opposition would not relent, while Rahul Gandhi climbed onto a barricade carrying the Tricolour and held a placard reading "Gyanesh, it's done bro!" Around 237 MPs participated in the march, according to Congress MP Manickam Tagore. The Biju Janata Dal, which is not part of the alliance, joined the march; the Aam Aadmi Party and the DMK did not take part.

## Sit-in at Akashvani Bhawan

After being briefly detained and taken to a police bus, Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra got off the bus and entered the premises of Akashvani Bhawan, the All India Radio headquarters located next to the Election Commission office, and sat under a tree in protest. They later staged a sit-in outside the building, insisting they would not leave without meeting the CEC. The ECI's Nirvachan Bhavan is about 270 metres from Akashvani.

## BJP responds

BJP spokesperson Sambit Patra accused the opposition bloc of attempting to create anarchy and of trying to "relaunch Rahul Gandhi" through the protest. He said the Supreme Court had merely issued notices in the CEC matter and had not declared Form 6 illegal. Congress MP Rajeev Shukla countered that it was the BJP spokesperson who had shamed public discourse by referring to the CEC as "Ganesh Kumar", comparing Lord Ganesh to a person accused of wrongdoing.
