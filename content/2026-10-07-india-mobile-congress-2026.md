India Mobile Congress 2026 Opens in New Delhi, PM Modi to Inaugurate on October 8

The 10th edition of the India Mobile Congress (IMC) began on Wednesday at Yashobhoomi in Dwarka, New Delhi, and will run through October 10. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to formally inaugurate the four-day event on October 8.

Jointly organised by the Department of Telecommunications and the Cellular Operators Association of India, this year's edition carries the theme "Scale Without Boundaries". The event is expected to draw more than 1.5 lakh attendees, over 500 exhibitors and partners, more than 600 startups and over 900 thought leaders, with delegations from over 100 countries.

Union Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia reviewed final preparations at the venue ahead of the opening and said the event would demonstrate India's telecom capabilities as the country seeks to move from providing services to developing technology products.

## Agenda and key sessions

The programme spans 6G, artificial intelligence, AI-native telecom networks, 5G monetisation, satellite communications, semiconductors, quantum technologies, cybersecurity and robotics. Scheduled highlights include the third International 6G Symposium, CEO and CTO roundtables, the Startup World Cup India 2026, a cybersecurity summit and over 700 curated investor-startup meetings. Industry leaders expected on stage include Reliance Jio chairman Akash Ambani, Bharti Enterprises chairman Sunil Bharti Mittal and Aditya Birla Group chairman Kumar Mangalam Birla, alongside executives from Qualcomm, Ericsson, Jio, Vodafone Idea, Airtel and Intel, and SpaceX Starlink's Lauren Dreyer.
