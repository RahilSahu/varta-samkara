Netflix to cut around 1,000 jobs in biggest layoffs since 2022

Netflix is cutting around 1,000 jobs, or about 6 per cent of its workforce, in its biggest round of layoffs since 2022, amid intense competition in the streaming market.

## Why it is happening

The cuts come as the streaming giant faces a fiercely competitive market and follow its loss to Paramount Skydance in the bidding for Warner Bros Discovery's streaming and studio assets, according to reports.

Source: The Hindu BusinessLine, Times Now
