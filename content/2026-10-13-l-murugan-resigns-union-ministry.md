L. Murugan Resigns as Union Minister and Rajya Sabha Member to Return to Grassroots BJP Work in Tamil Nadu

Union Minister of State L. Murugan resigned on Tuesday from the Union Council of Ministers and his Rajya Sabha membership, writing to Prime Minister Narendra Modi that he wants to return to the grassroots and dedicate himself full-time to strengthening the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) in Tamil Nadu ahead of the 2029 Lok Sabha elections.

## The resignation

Murugan, who held the portfolios of Information and Broadcasting and Parliamentary Affairs as a Minister of State, announced his decision in a post on X, sharing his resignation letter addressed to the Prime Minister.

## His stated reason

He said it had been the greatest honour of his life to serve under the Prime Minister, and added that he now wished to return to the grassroots, stand with party workers, and dedicate himself full-time to strengthening the BJP in Tamil Nadu. He closed his post with the words: "Nation First. Party Next. Self Last."

## Political context

The move comes amid speculation about a reshuffle in the Union Cabinet. Reports noted that the speculation had been building after several Rajya Sabha MPs and ministers, including Petroleum Minister Hardeep Singh Puri, were not renominated. The Prime Minister's Office has not yet announced whether the resignation has been accepted.
