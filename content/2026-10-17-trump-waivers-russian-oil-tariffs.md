Trump Waives Sanctions Law That Threatened 100% Tariffs on Russian-Oil Buyers Including India

US President Donald Trump has issued waivers on a sanctions law that gave the administration authority to impose up to 100% tariffs on countries importing Russian-origin crude oil or natural gas, including India.

## What changed

The move came nearly six days after Trump allowed Russian diesel imports into the United States. The waivers cover the law that sought to penalise countries buying Russian crude or gas, with the stipulated tariffs having been set to take effect on October 18 to tighten the flow of oil-related revenues to Russia.

The decision follows an agreement announced between Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin under which Moscow will supply diesel to help stabilise global energy markets.

## Relief for India

India, a major buyer of discounted Russian crude, had faced the threat of steep tariffs over its energy trade with Moscow. The waiver offers significant relief to Indian refiners and to the ongoing trade negotiations between New Delhi and Washington.
