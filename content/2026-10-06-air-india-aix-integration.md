Air India Group Weighs Merging Key Functions of Air India and Air India Express

The Air India Group is considering integrating key departments such as human resources, finance, information technology and engineering across Air India and Air India Express, in a bid to cut duplication and costs, sources familiar with the development said. The two airlines would keep their separate brands and business models even if the plan goes ahead.

The move comes as the group looks for savings and efficiency across its operations. Combined losses for the group in the financial year 2025-26 stood at Rs 22,238 crore, sharpening the focus on reducing overheads.

## What the plan covers

Under the proposal being discussed, back-office functions including HR, finance, IT and engineering would be brought under a shared structure serving both carriers. Commercial and customer-facing operations would continue to run as two distinct airlines, with Air India operating as the full-service carrier and Air India Express as the low-cost arm.

No formal decision has been announced yet, and the proposal is still under consideration within the group.

## Why it matters now

Air India's turnaround under the Tata Group has involved fleet expansion, network growth and large hiring, all of which have weighed on the group's finances. Integrating overlapping functions is a standard step airlines take to control costs, and industry watchers say it could help the group protect margins while it continues to invest in aircraft and routes.
