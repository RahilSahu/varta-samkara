Air India Frankfurt-Delhi Flight Diverted to Jaipur After False Hijack Alert, DGCA Orders Probe

Air India flight AI2030 from Frankfurt to Delhi was diverted to Jaipur on Wednesday after a hijack alert that later turned out to be false, officials said, prompting a probe by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation into the incident.

## What happened on the flight

The Boeing 787-9 aircraft, carrying around 293 passengers and 13 crew members, landed at Jaipur International Airport at about 9:35 am and was moved to an isolation bay for security checks. Security sources said the Pilot-in-Command had transmitted Code 7500, the internationally recognised transponder code for unlawful interference, and that the code was activated due to a technical glitch. After inspections, the passengers were brought to the terminal building and later reboarded, and the flight departed for Delhi.

## Minister waits for the probe report

Civil Aviation Minister K Rammohan Naidu said on Thursday that a decision on whether changes are needed in existing security norms can be taken only after the DGCA completes its investigation. "First (we need to) find out what exactly happened. So, once we know what exactly happened, then only we can see what changes are required... we will just wait until DGCA gives final information," Naidu told reporters on the sidelines of a function marking the 42nd Raising Day of Pawan Hans in New Delhi.
