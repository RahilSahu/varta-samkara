TSMC Posts Record Third-Quarter Profit for Sixth Straight Quarter on AI Chip Demand

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co reported a record third-quarter net profit of NT$763.1 billion, about $23.5 billion, up 68.7 per cent from a year earlier, marking its sixth consecutive quarter of record earnings on surging demand for artificial intelligence chips.

## The numbers

Revenue reached a record NT$1.49 trillion, about $46.4 billion, up about 50.9 per cent year-on-year. The net profit beat the LSEG SmartEstimate of NT$746.3 billion compiled from 16 analysts. High-performance computing accounted for 68 per cent of revenue, underscoring TSMC's role as the key supplier to Nvidia and Apple. The company's market capitalisation stands at about $2 trillion, nearly double that of South Korean rival Samsung Electronics.

## Investing through the cycle

TSMC is investing $265 billion to build chip factories in the US state of Arizona, including an additional $100 billion unveiled in July, and has been evaluating a potential investment in Texas. On its July earnings call, the company raised this year's capital expenditure forecast to between $60 billion and $64 billion. Its Taipei-listed shares have gained 57 per cent so far this year.

Source: Reuters, The Standard (Hong Kong)
