FIRs Registered Against Rahul, Priyanka Gandhi After INDIA Bloc Protest Against CEC at Jantar Mantar

Delhi Police on Thursday analysed mobile-phone videos showing its personnel, including women officers and constables, allegedly assaulted during the INDIA bloc's protest near Jantar Mantar, with sources saying FIRs were being registered against Rahul Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi and others under provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita dealing with assault on public servants and obstructing public servants in the discharge of their duties.

The protest, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over allegations of vote theft, entered its second day with dramatic scenes. Rahul Gandhi led a procession of buses from a Congress lunch hosted by Mallikarjun Kharge to near Jantar Mantar, where police stalled the march midway and forcibly detained him and other leaders. Congress MP Vamsi Krishna Gaddam alleged that Rahul and Priyanka were attacked and fellow MPs were lathi-charged, questioning the Z-category security granted to the CEC while opposition representatives faced detention.

The INDIA bloc leaders later held a sit-in at Rajghat, where Gaurav Gogoi said the country was united in demanding Kumar's resignation and urged the Prime Minister to listen. The protest concluded in the evening with leaders leaving Rajghat. Separately, the bloc held a massive march in Patna led by Tejashwi Yadav as part of a statewide agitation against the CEC.

## Heightened security ahead of October 10 protest

More than 20,000 police and paramilitary personnel have been deployed across Delhi ahead of the CJP's October 10 protest at Jantar Mantar, a senior officer said. CJP founder Abhijeet Dipke said the protest would go ahead despite the possibility of detention, stating they were ready to be detained or arrested. Rebel TMC MPs aligned with the NCPI also sought 15 more days to reply to the Lok Sabha Speaker's notices on their disqualification under the anti-defection law.
