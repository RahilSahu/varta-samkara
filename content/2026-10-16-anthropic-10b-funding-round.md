Anthropic Reportedly in Talks to Raise $10 Billion at $350 Billion Valuation

Anthropic is reportedly in talks to raise $10 billion in a new funding round that would value the Claude chatbot maker at $350 billion, according to Bloomberg, with Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC and Coatue expected to lead the round.

## The terms

The round would nearly double Anthropic's valuation from its $183 billion Series F just three months ago, when it raised $13 billion. Over the past year the company has raised capital at a rapid pace, securing $3.5 billion at a $61.5 billion valuation in March. Microsoft and Nvidia are also expected to participate in the round. The deal is expected to close in the coming weeks, though sources cautioned that the final amount could change. Anthropic has not commented on the reported fundraising.

## Separate from the compute deal

The fundraising is separate from the $15 billion recently committed by Nvidia and Microsoft, which underpins an arrangement in which Anthropic would buy up to $30 billion in compute from Microsoft Azure running on Nvidia chips. The new equity would support hiring, research and expansion rather than infrastructure purchases.
