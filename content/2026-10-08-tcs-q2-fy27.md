TCS Q2: Profit Up 15% to Rs 13,884 Crore, AI Revenue Crosses $3.1 Billion Annualised

Tata Consultancy Services on Thursday reported a 15 per cent year-on-year rise in consolidated net profit to Rs 13,884 crore for the September quarter of FY27, with revenue from operations up 11 per cent to Rs 73,188 crore. Sequentially, profit rose 4 per cent and revenue 1.3 per cent. Operating margin stood at 24 per cent and net margin at 19 per cent.

The board declared a second interim dividend of Rs 12 per equity share for FY27, with the record date fixed as October 14 and payment due by October 30. Annualised AI revenue crossed $3.1 billion in the quarter, topping 10 per cent of overall revenue, while total contract value came in at $9.6 billion. CEO K Krithivasan cited broad-based growth across international markets and segments, and pointed to new transformation partnerships with Porsche and Best Buy.

## Workforce and market context

TCS ended the quarter with 598,056 employees, with attrition stable at 13.3 per cent. The result arrives amid pressure on Indian IT stocks from weak discretionary spending and AI disruption concerns; TCS shares closed marginally lower at Rs 2,075.25 on the BSE ahead of the announcement. Analysts focused on constant-currency growth, BFSI recovery and the deal pipeline as signals of whether large deals and AI-led work are holding up.
