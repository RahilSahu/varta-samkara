BRICS Traditional and Indigenous Demonstration Games Held in Ahmedabad
The BRICS Traditional and Indigenous Demonstration Games 2026 were held on Monday at the Veer Savarkar Sports Complex in Naranpura, Ahmedabad, with delegations from 10 BRICS nations taking part in a day long non competitive showcase.
## India proposals
Declaring the games open, Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya said India is placing sport at the centre of nation building and proposed three areas of cooperation: a voluntary BRICS repository of traditional and indigenous sports, exchanges among practitioners, coaches and researchers, and periodic demonstrations at future BRICS sporting events. Gujarat Deputy Chief Minister Harsh Sanghavi was present at the inauguration, PIB said.
## Demonstrations
India presented Mallakhamb, Yogasana and Kalaripayattu. Other demonstrations included Wushu and Lion Dance from China, Capoeira from Brazil, Gorodki and Sambo from Russia, and Kgati and Iintonga from South Africa, among others listed by PIB. Teams were felicitated at the conclusion of the programme.
