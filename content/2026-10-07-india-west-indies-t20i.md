Shreyas Iyer's maiden T20I century powers India to 8-wicket win over West Indies in Lucknow

India beat West Indies by eight wickets in the first T20I of the five-match series at Lucknow's Ekana Cricket Stadium on Tuesday, taking a 1-0 lead. Captain Shreyas Iyer produced an unbeaten 102 from 43 balls, his first hundred in T20 internationals, as India chased down 172 with 32 balls to spare.

India won the toss and chose to bowl. West Indies were dismissed for 171 in 19.1 overs. Shai Hope made 52 off 37 balls and Sherfane Rutherford countered with 56 off 34, but wickets fell steadily after their partnership. Debutant Naman Dhir was the pick of India's bowlers with three for 31 in four overs, while Axar Patel took two for 26 and Kuldeep Yadav claimed two wickets.

## The chase

India stumbled early, losing Abhishek Sharma and Sanju Samson to sit at 35 for two. Iyer then took charge alongside Ishan Kishan, who made an unbeaten 37 off 27. The pair added 137 runs for the third wicket. Iyer reached his century off 42 balls with a six off Roston Chase and finished with 10 fours and six sixes at a strike rate above 237.

The result extends India's momentum after their ODI series win over the same opposition. Player of the Match went to Iyer. The two sides now move to Ranchi for the second T20I on Friday, October 9.
