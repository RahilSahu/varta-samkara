Jannik Sinner Shuts Down 2026 Season to Treat Knee Injury

World number one Jannik Sinner has announced he will miss the rest of the 2026 tennis season to recover from a knee injury, the Italian said on October 6. He has not played since beating Alexander Zverev in the Wimbledon final on July 12, when he defended his title and claimed a fifth Grand Slam crown.

In a video posted on social media, the 25-year-old said that he and his doctors had decided he needed a course of treatment for his knee, which meant the 2026 season was officially over. His team said he would follow a course of treatment for inflammation in his knee.

## What he will miss

Sinner has already missed the North American hard-court swing, the US Open and the Asian tournaments. He will now also sit out the European indoor hard-court swing, including the Paris Masters, and the season-ending ATP Finals in Turin, where he won the title in the last two years. He will also be unavailable for Italy, the host nation and three-time defending champion, at the Davis Cup Final 8.

## Rankings at stake

His absence could open the door for Zverev, the world number two, to finish the year at the top of the rankings. Zverev has cut the gap to Sinner after winning the French Open and US Open titles this year.

Sinner's team said the decision had been made to have the treatment now to give him the necessary time to recover before beginning preparations for 2027. If he returns at the start of next year, the Australian Open, beginning January 17, would be his next possible Grand Slam.
