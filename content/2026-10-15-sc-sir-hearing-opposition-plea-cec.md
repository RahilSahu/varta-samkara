Supreme Court to hear Opposition plea challenging SIR voter deletions today

The Supreme Court is scheduled to hear a joint petition filed by Opposition leaders led by Rahul Gandhi on Thursday, seeking a full disclosure of Election Commission records on the special intensive revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, which has so far deleted about 13 crore voters across India.

## What the petition seeks

The petition seeks "files, agendas, minutes, notings and objections" recorded by Election Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi on the revision exercise. Filed on October 12, it also asks for booth-wise details of deleted voters, a guarantee that no voter is removed without notice and a fair hearing, and a halt to the ongoing revision until the court decides the matter.

## Why it matters

The SIR has become the most contested electoral exercise of the year, with Opposition parties alleging wrongful deletions and protesters in Delhi demanding the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The hearing puts the Commission's internal records and the revision process under direct judicial scrutiny.
