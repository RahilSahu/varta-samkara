Government Revises Fuel Export Duties From October 16: Petrol Levy Raised, Diesel Cut

The government has revised windfall taxes on fuel exports with effect from October 16, raising the export duty on petrol to Rs 2.5 per litre from Rs 0.5 per litre, while cutting the duty on diesel exports to Rs 8.5 per litre from Rs 16 per litre, according to a government order issued late on Thursday.

## Why the levies were changed

The order, reported by Reuters, adjusts the fortnightly export levies in line with movements in international crude oil and petroleum product prices. India first imposed windfall taxes on fuel exports in July 2022 and withdrew them two years later. The government reintroduced the tax in March 2026 after crude prices jumped during the US-Israeli war on Iran.

## How the review works

The export duties are currently reviewed every fortnight and adjusted in line with global crude benchmarks and refined product prices, the report said.
