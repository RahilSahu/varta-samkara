Centre gives airlines more time on Navi Mumbai airport shift, October 26 deadline under review

The government has agreed to give airlines more time to negotiate the proposed shift of international flights from Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport to Navi Mumbai International Airport. Weekly departure slots at Mumbai airport will not be discontinued from October 25 or 26 as originally envisaged, and airlines will get additional time to work out the transition.

The development follows a meeting chaired by the Civil Aviation Secretary with Adani Airports Holdings, the Airports Authority of India and the Federation of Indian Airlines, including Air India, IndiGo and SpiceJet. Mumbai International Airport Limited, operated by the Adani group, had proposed temporarily discontinuing 265 of its 770 weekly international departures, about 34.4 per cent, from October 26, with impacted airlines asked to move those operations to Navi Mumbai.

## Why airlines pushed back

Carriers told the civil aviation ministry that shifting within the proposed timeframe would be expensive and could lead to financial losses, noting that several passengers had already booked tickets for flights departing from Mumbai. The International Air Transport Association had described the move as a "forced relocation" in a letter last month, saying the transition plan was not devised in consultation with airlines.

An extension of the October 26 deadline is under consideration, although a revised date is yet to be decided. Additional incentives for airlines operating from Navi Mumbai have been proposed, along with the possibility of levying the same user development fee at both airports, a point of dispute since Navi Mumbai's fee is higher.

Under the original plan, the capacity freed at Mumbai airport's Terminal 2 would be used to move domestic traffic from Terminal 1, accommodating around five million domestic passengers a year. Airlines will now hold further talks with Adani Airports and submit proposals to the ministry, which will use the inputs to finalise the transition plan.
