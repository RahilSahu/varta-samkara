India's trade deficit with China crosses 100 billion dollars in 2026

India's trade deficit with China has crossed 100 billion dollars this year, according to data released on Wednesday by China's General Administration of Customs (GAC). India's exports to China grew 40% in the first three quarters to 20 billion dollars, although the deficit continued to widen.

## The numbers

The 40% rise in exports to China in the first three quarters took the figure to 20 billion dollars. Despite this growth, imports from China outpaced exports by a wide margin, pushing the deficit beyond the 100-billion-dollar mark for the year.

## Trade relations

The figures were released amid ongoing efforts by both countries to manage the economic relationship. India has been seeking to diversify its trade partnerships and reduce dependence on Chinese imports, while engaging with other major economies on trade deals.
