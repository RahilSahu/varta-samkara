India-UAE Joint Military Exercise Mountain Guardian 2026 Begins in Uttarakhand

The third edition of the India-UAE joint military exercise, Mountain Guardian 2026, began on Monday at the Foreign Training Node in Chaubatia, Uttarakhand. The two-week exercise will run from October 5 to October 18, 2026, with a focus on mountain warfare.

A total of 92 personnel, with equal representation from both armies, are taking part. The Indian Army contingent comprises troops from a Bhairav Battalion, while the UAE Army is represented by troops from a Mountain Infantry Battalion. Women officers and combatants from both armies are also participating in specialist roles.

## Training programme

The joint training schedule includes joint planning, tactical drills, firing practices, establishment of surveillance grids, drone operations, physical endurance training, rock climbing, obstacle training and integrated field exercises. The programme also incorporates technology-enabled operations and an exchange of tactics, techniques and procedures relevant to operations in challenging mountainous terrain.

## Deeper defence cooperation

The Defence Ministry said the exercise aims to enhance interoperability, mutual understanding and cooperation between the two armies. It will give both sides an opportunity to exchange professional experience, sharpen tactical proficiency and share best practices in operational procedures for sub-conventional operations in mountainous conditions.

Mountain Guardian is part of the expanding defence engagement between India and the UAE, and officials said it will further strengthen military-to-military cooperation and reinforce the bonds of friendship and trust between the two nations.
