India and Czech Republic Agree to Expand Defence Industrial Cooperation

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh held bilateral talks with the Czech Republic's First Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Industry and Trade, Karel Havlicek, in New Delhi on Tuesday, with the two sides agreeing to expand defence industrial cooperation, including through joint ventures and the India-Czech Joint Defence Committee.

## What was discussed

The two leaders reviewed the full spectrum of defence industrial cooperation between the two countries, according to the Defence Ministry. They welcomed the ongoing collaboration and the successful establishment of several joint ventures.

## Make in India link

Both sides agreed to further strengthen bilateral cooperation to promote the Make in India initiative and the government's push for Aatmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliant India). The India-Czech Joint Defence Committee will remain a key platform for advancing the partnership and identifying areas for deeper industrial collaboration.

## Wider context

The discussions also covered peace and security issues, with both sides acknowledging the need for greater cooperation to maintain regional stability. The two leaders said the partnership was rooted in historical and cultural ties and shared values of mutual trust and respect. Havlicek is on a four-day visit to India that also includes meetings with ministries, trade bodies and businesses, and visits to industries in Bengaluru and Mumbai.
