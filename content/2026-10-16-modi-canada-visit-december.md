PM Modi to Visit Canada on December 11 and 12, First Bilateral Trip Since 2015

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Canada on December 11 and 12, the High Commission of India in Ottawa said on Friday, following a telephone conversation between Modi and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney earlier this week.

## What both sides agreed

In a post on X, the High Commission said both leaders agreed to build on the positive momentum in ties and work towards concrete outcomes in trade, energy, technology, security and people-to-people ties. Carney confirmed the visit while speaking to the Indo-Canadian community at a gathering in Calgary on Thursday, saying the visit would likely include Ottawa and Toronto.

## Trade talks gather pace

India and Canada are speeding up negotiations on a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement, with the fifth round of trade talks concluded in Ottawa last week. According to the Canadian Prime Minister's Office, both leaders reaffirmed their commitment to conclude negotiations at the earliest opportunity, supporting efforts to double two-way trade by 2030.

## A reset in relations

India and Canada have been working to improve ties since Carney became Prime Minister in 2025. This will be Modi's first bilateral trip to Canada since 2015, when he visited Ottawa, Toronto and Vancouver. According to people familiar with his schedule, Modi will meet Carney in Ottawa, interact with Canadian businesses and attend a gathering with the Indian diaspora in Toronto, before travelling to Miami for the G20 Leaders' Summit.
