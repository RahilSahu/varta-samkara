ED Pastes Summons at Ex-Kerala CM Pinarayi Vijayan's Residence in CMRL Money-Laundering Case

The Enforcement Directorate on Saturday pasted a copy of a summons outside the residence of former Keralam Chief Minister and Leader of Opposition Pinarayi Vijayan in the CMRL-linked money-laundering case, asking him to appear for questioning on October 23.

## The summons

The summons was issued on Friday under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), under which the agency has registered a case after taking cognisance of a state police FIR in the matter. Vijayan, 81, has been asked to depose at the ED's zonal office in Kochi.

ED officials had earlier sent the notice by email and attempted to serve it in person at his Thiruvananthapuram residence. Finding he was not home, and after household staff declined to accept the notice twice, the officials formally served it by pasting it outside the residence.

## The case so far

The Keralam Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau registered FIRs against Vijayan, his daughter T. Veena, and son-in-law and former minister P.A. Mohamed Riyas after the Kerala High Court on October 9 directed the VACB to register a case based on a report submitted by the ED. The ED has alleged that Cochin Minerals and Rutile Limited (CMRL) made fraudulent payments to Veena's now-defunct company, Exalogic Solutions, under the guise of IT consultancy services.

Vijayan moved the Supreme Court on Friday challenging the High Court order. His lawyer has indicated he will reply to the ED, citing the ongoing assembly session, which concludes on October 29, as the reason for his inability to appear on the date.
