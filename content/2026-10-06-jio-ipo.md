Jio Platforms IPO to Open on October 21, Listing Likely on October 28

The initial public offering of Jio Platforms, which is expected to be the country's biggest issue, is likely to open for subscription on October 21, sources aware of the development said. The shares are likely to be listed on the stock exchanges on October 28.

The issue will open for subscription from October 21 to October 23 after the completion of the anchor round on October 19. The company is likely to get listed at an enterprise valuation of 143 to 146 billion US dollars, or above Rs 12 lakh crore. An email query sent to Jio Platforms did not elicit a reply.

## Issue details

The IPO is estimated at around 3.8 billion US dollars. If completed at that size, it would become India's largest public offer, surpassing Hyundai Motor India's 2.9 billion dollar listing in 2024. The company filed its draft IPO papers in June, and SEBI issued its final observations on August 28.

According to the draft prospectus, the company plans to issue up to 27 crore fresh equity shares, equivalent to about 2.9 per cent of its post-issue equity base. The company has concluded overseas roadshows and is expected to file final papers with the market regulator in the week starting October 12.

## Market context

Proceeds from the offering will largely be used to repay debt owed by the company's telecoms division. Jio Platforms, which counts Meta and Google among its major foreign investors, also runs AI, cloud and enterprise network businesses.

The IPO comes as India's primary market has seen heightened activity. More than two dozen IPOs have been announced or launched since July 1, nearly matching the 28 recorded in the first half of 2026, and almost 250 companies are in the pipeline to raise a combined Rs 4.65 lakh crore, according to PRIME Database.
