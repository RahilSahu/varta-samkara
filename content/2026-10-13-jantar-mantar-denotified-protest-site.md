Delhi Police De-notifies Jantar Mantar as Designated Protest Site, Names Three Alternative Venues

The Delhi Police on Monday de-notified Jantar Mantar as a designated venue for protests and demonstrations in the national capital, designating Ramlila Ground, Burari Ground and CBD Ground in East Delhi as the new venues for protests. The move partially modifies a standing order issued in April 2022.

## The official order

The order was issued by Delhi Police Commissioner Anurag Kumar on October 11. It states that the Union Ministry of Home Affairs had constituted a committee to examine alternative venues for citizens to exercise their right to protest, and that the change follows the committee's recommendations. Revised standard operating procedures for demonstrations at the three new grounds will be issued separately.

## Court proceedings

The development comes days after the Supreme Court, on October 9, recorded that suitable alternative sites for protests needed to be identified and designated. In the Delhi High Court on Tuesday, Additional Solicitor General Chetan Sharma, appearing for the government, argued that a petition by Indian Youth Congress president Uday Bhanu Chib challenging the denial of permission to protest at Jantar Mantar had been rendered infructuous since the site now stands denotified. The petition also challenges the broader practice of denying permission to protest at designated sites.

## Reactions

Activists criticised the decision, saying that relocating demonstrations away from the seat of power would make it harder for citizens to draw the attention of political leaders and decision-makers to their demands. Jantar Mantar has long been one of the capital's best-known protest sites because of its proximity to Parliament, government offices and the media. Police, on their part, have said the new framework follows the Home Ministry's committee recommendations. The earlier standing order had restricted protests at Jantar Mantar to the hours between 10 a.m. and 5 p.m. and did not permit a programme to continue for more than a day at a time.
