Two Pakistan Army Pilots Killed as Training Aircraft Crashes Near Quetta
Two Pakistan Army Aviation pilots were killed on Monday when a Mushshak training aircraft crashed near Nawan Killi in Quetta district, the military media wing ISPR said.
## Details released by ISPR
The aircraft crashed around midday on October 12 due to a technical fault, ISPR said. The pilots were identified as Major Husnain Shehzad Humayun, an instructor pilot, and Captain Abdul Samad, a student pilot. No further cause was given in the statement.
## Condolences
President Asif Ali Zardari and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed grief and offered condolences to the families, according to official statements reported in Pakistani media. ISPR noted earlier training related crashes in June, including an air force aircraft crash in Mardan and an army helicopter crash near Muzaffarabad.
