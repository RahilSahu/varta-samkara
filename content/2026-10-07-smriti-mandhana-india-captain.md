Smriti Mandhana Named India Women's Cricket Captain as Harmanpreet Kaur Steps Down

The BCCI on Tuesday announced Smriti Mandhana as the new captain of the Indian women's cricket team, hours after Harmanpreet Kaur stepped down from the leadership role across formats. Harmanpreet, who led India to the 2025 ODI World Cup title, remains available as a player and has been included in the squads.

Reports said the decision followed a BCCI review meeting in Guwahati that assessed recent performances, including India's exit from the T20 World Cup before the semifinals. In her announcement, Harmanpreet wrote: "Today, I step down as captain of the Indian Women's Cricket Team across all formats."

Mandhana, 29, has been Harmanpreet's deputy and part of the Indian team since 2013. She has previously led India in 18 T20Is and four ODIs, and has franchise leadership experience, having guided Royal Challengers Bengaluru to Women's Premier League titles in 2024 and 2026. She was the second-highest run-scorer at the 2025 World Cup with 434 runs and became the first Indian woman to score international hundreds in all three formats with a T20I century against England in June 2025. The BCCI said she brings considerable experience into the role as the team enters a new phase.

## First assignment: Zimbabwe series

Her tenure begins with the upcoming three-match T20I and three-match ODI series against Zimbabwe, for which the BCCI named new-look squads on Tuesday. Shafali Verma was named vice-captain for the T20Is and Deepti Sharma for the ODIs, with Harmanpreet continuing in both squads.
