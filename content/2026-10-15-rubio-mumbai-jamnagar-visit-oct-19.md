Marco Rubio to visit Mumbai and Jamnagar next week, skipping Delhi

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio will travel to India from October 19 to 22 for his second visit in six months, with energy and trade relations on the agenda as he visits Mumbai and Jamnagar, the US Department of State announced on Wednesday. Rubio will skip Delhi on this trip.

## Visit itinerary

Rubio travels to India after attending a regional conference in Samarkand, Uzbekistan. The State Department's announcement underlined energy and trade as the top items for the visit, which comes as both countries work to deepen economic ties.
