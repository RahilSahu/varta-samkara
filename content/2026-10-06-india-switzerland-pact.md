India and Switzerland Sign Migration and Mobility Partnership During Swiss President Guy Parmelin's Visit

India and Switzerland signed a Migration and Mobility Partnership (MMP) on Monday during the state visit of Swiss President Guy Parmelin, who held talks with Prime Minister Narendra Modi at Hyderabad House in New Delhi. The pact anchors a set of five cooperation instruments covering migration, young professionals, transport infrastructure, scientific research and startup development, according to the Ministry of External Affairs.

## What the mobility pact covers

The MoU on the Migration and Mobility Partnership aims to develop cooperation under equal treatment principles and to prevent irregular migration, human exploitation and trafficking, in line with national laws. It provides for multiple-entry visas valid for up to five years, with stays of up to six months on each visit, and renewable one-year student permits. A separate Agreement on the Exchange of Young Professionals will allow 300 young professionals from each country to take up temporary jobs in the other country every year, a number that may be raised to 500, with nationality and residency verification timelines of 30 to 90 days.

## Trade, defence and nuclear energy

In his media statement, PM Modi said the two sides had identified new possibilities for cooperation in military exchanges and defence production and had reached a consensus on deepening collaboration in nuclear energy. He said a series of decisions were taken to expand market access for Indian exports to Switzerland and facilitate investment between the two countries, and that the new cooperation would create one million direct jobs in India over the next 15 years. The two sides also signed an MoU on transportation technologies, mobility systems and infrastructure covering electric vehicles, ropeways, tunnelling and the circular economy.

## EFTA momentum

The leaders noted the momentum from the India-EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA), operationalised last October, which PM Modi described as India's first free trade agreement with an economic bloc in Europe. The Swiss side has projected $100 billion in investment linked to the growing partnership, ANI reported.
