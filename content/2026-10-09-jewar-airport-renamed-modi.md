UP Cabinet Renames Noida's Jewar Airport as Narendra Modi International Airport

The Uttar Pradesh Cabinet on Friday approved renaming the Noida International Airport at Jewar as the Narendra Modi International Airport, Noida-Jewar. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath announced the decision, saying the cabinet was grateful to Prime Minister Narendra Modi for what he described as a major gift to the state.

"The Council of Ministers of Uttar Pradesh expresses its gratitude to Prime Minister Narendra Modi for gifting the state the Noida International Airport at Jewar. The Cabinet has approved a proposal to name it Narendra Modi International Airport, Noida-Jewar," Adityanath said. He added that the airport was set to emerge as Uttar Pradesh's largest airport and a major aviation hub.

Prime Minister Modi inaugurated the airport on March 28 this year, and its first commercial landing, an IndiGo flight from Lucknow, took place on June 15. Developed by Yamuna International Airport Private Limited, a subsidiary of Swiss firm Zurich Airport International AG, with the state-backed Noida International Airport Limited as implementing agency, the project cost Rs 11,282 crore in its first phase. Phase one has an initial annual capacity of 1.2 crore passengers, scalable to 7 crore, with a 3,900-metre runway capable of handling wide-body aircraft.
