Tesla to unveil next-generation Roadster tonight after nine years of delays

Tesla will stream the reveal of its next-generation Roadster live tonight at 7:55pm CT from the SpaceX test site in McGregor, Texas, bringing an end to nine years of delays for the long-promised sports car.

## What to expect

Reports say the outdoor demonstration may feature a hovering or flying display using SpaceX cold gas thrusters. Production is now targeted for 2027 or 2028.
