Cabinet Renames NSAP as Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana, Approves Rs 53,428 Crore Outlay

The Union Cabinet on Wednesday approved the continuation of the National Social Assistance Programme for five years, from 2026-27 to 2030-31, with a total outlay of Rs 53,428 crore, renaming the scheme the Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana.

## New names for the sub-schemes

The scheme and its components will be renamed under the new title. The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme becomes the Pradhan Mantri Vridhavastha Pension Yojana, the widow pension scheme becomes the Pradhan Mantri Vidhawa Pension Yojana, the disability pension scheme becomes the Pradhan Mantri Divyangjan Pension Yojana, and the National Family Benefit Scheme becomes the Pradhan Mantri Parivar Labh Yojana.

## Disability pension norms eased

The benchmark disability requirement for the pension has been cut from 80 per cent to 40 per cent, and the minimum age criterion has been removed, making persons with disabilities eligible from birth. Monthly assistance will be Rs 375 for beneficiaries aged up to 79 years and Rs 625 for those aged 80 and above. Briefing the media, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said disability pension coverage would expand from 8.33 lakh to as many as 27 lakh beneficiaries.

## Coverage and continuity

The Cabinet's approval is expected to benefit about 3.20 crore beneficiaries across all 36 states and Union territories. The old-age and widow pension schemes and the family benefit scheme will continue in their existing form. Revised guidelines for the disability pension will be issued within three months.
