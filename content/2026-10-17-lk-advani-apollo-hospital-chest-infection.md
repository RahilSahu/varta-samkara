LK Advani Admitted to Delhi's Apollo Hospital With Chest Infection, Condition Stable

Veteran Bharatiya Janata Party leader and former Deputy Prime Minister Lal Krishna Advani has been admitted to Apollo Hospital in New Delhi with a chest infection.

## Hospitalisation

The 98-year-old leader was taken to the hospital around 7:30 pm on Friday (October 16) after complaining of a chest infection. Hospital officials said he is under observation and his condition is stable.

BJP president and Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda visited the hospital to check on the veteran leader.

## Response

Advani, one of the founding figures of the BJP, has largely withdrawn from active politics in recent years but remains the party's senior-most living leader. There has been no further official medical bulletin as of Saturday morning.
