Modi inaugurates Rs 3,350 crore development projects in Ladakh

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated five major development projects worth around Rs 3,350 crore in Ladakh on Thursday via video conferencing, covering airport infrastructure, strategic road connectivity, hydropower, heritage conservation and water security.

## Airport and strategic road

The projects include a new passenger terminal building at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport in Leh, built at a cost of Rs 640 crore. Spread over 23,800 square metres and designed to handle 35 lakh passengers annually, the terminal incorporates geothermal energy to improve energy efficiency and reduce its carbon footprint.

The 298-km Nimmu-Padum-Darcha road provides alternative connectivity to Ladakh and strengthens strategic mobility for the armed forces.

## Heritage conservation

The Prime Minister also inaugurated India's first petroglyph conservation park in Leh, dedicated to the preservation of Ladakh's ancient archaeological heritage.
