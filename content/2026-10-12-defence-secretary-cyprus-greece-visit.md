Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh Begins Cyprus and Greece Visit from October 12 to 15

Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh began an official visit to Cyprus and Greece from October 12 to 15 to strengthen defence and strategic cooperation, with maritime security and defence industrial cooperation as prominent themes, an official statement said.

## Cyprus leg

In Cyprus on October 12 and 13, Singh is scheduled to meet counterparts in the Cypriot defence ministry and call on the country leadership. The visit follows the President of Cyprus visit to India in May 2026, after which the India and Cyprus strategic partnership gained momentum, the statement noted.

## Greece leg and wider context

In Greece, Singh will interact with senior defence and military leadership. The Greek defence minister visited India in February 2026 for talks with Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. Officials said the tour also follows the India and EU Security and Defence Partnership and is expected to give further impetus to defence engagement with both countries.
