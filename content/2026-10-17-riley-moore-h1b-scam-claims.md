US Congressman Riley Moore Calls H-1B and OPT Programmes "Scams", Claims Indians and Chinese Replacing Americans

US Congressman Riley Moore has alleged that H-1B visa holders from India and China are replacing American computer science graduates, calling the H-1B and Optional Practical Training (OPT) programmes "scams" that let employers hire cheaper foreign labour.

## The claims

In a post on X on October 16, Moore wrote: "H-1B and OPT programs are SCAMS that undercut and replace American workers with cheap foreign labor." He claimed that around 650,000 Americans graduated with computer science-related degrees last year, while more than 500,000 H-1B visas were issued, mostly to holders from India and China.

Speaking on The Scott Jennings Show on the Salem News Channel, Moore argued the programme was sold as bringing "the best and the brightest" to fill jobs Americans could not do, but was instead being used to hire cheaper labour. He also claimed a typical H-1B holder brings ten family members with them.

## The debate

The remarks come days after US Vice President JD Vance described H-1B workers as "foreign indentured servants", intensifying a debate that directly affects Indian professionals, who account for the majority of H-1B beneficiaries. Independent research, however, has disputed claims that H-1B workers systematically earn less than American workers in similar jobs.

Separately, the US Department of Homeland Security has proposed a rule that could make H-1B and L-1 visa extensions harder by removing the deference given to earlier approvals, a change DHS is seeking public feedback on.
