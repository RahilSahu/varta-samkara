Zelenskyy Says Russia Continued Energy Strikes Despite Trump Ceasefire Claim

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that Russia continued striking Ukrainian energy facilities after US President Donald Trump said a day earlier that both sides had agreed to an immediate energy ceasefire.

## Remarks in Warsaw

Speaking at the Warsaw Security Forum, Zelenskyy said Kyiv learned of the claimed agreement through media reports and would comply if Moscow did the same, but had not heard approval from the Russian side. He said facilities hit included a power plant in Kharkiv, a gas production facility and a civilian gas station. He also warned that disruption to Ukrainian grain exports by sea could affect food supplies in Africa, Asia and the Middle East.

## Other accounts

EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas welcomed the prospect of a pause but expressed doubt that Russia would honour it. Ukraine air force said it intercepted or suppressed 89 of 121 Russian drones overnight into Monday. Russia defence ministry said its air defences intercepted 151 Ukrainian drones overnight. Zelenskyy said Kyiv could halt strikes on Russian refineries if Moscow stopped targeting Ukraine energy system, and said elections would require at least a 60 day full ceasefire.
