Supreme Court mandates insurance on offline railway tickets from October 27

The Supreme Court has issued a mandatory directive that tickets sold at counters at every railway station across the country must include travel insurance coverage identical to that of online bookings, with the order to take effect from October 27, 2026.

## The court's order

A Bench of Justices Ahsanuddin Amanullah and R. Mahadevan directed the railway authorities to put the order into effect from the specified date, bringing offline passengers under the same insurance cover that online ticket buyers already receive.
