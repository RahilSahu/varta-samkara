Pakistan test-fires extended-range Fatah-2 hypersonic ballistic missile

The Pakistan Army on Tuesday conducted a successful test launch of the hypersonic ballistic missile Fatah-2 extended range (ER), its military media wing Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said in a statement.

## Weapon features

According to ISPR, the Fatah-2 ER is equipped with advanced navigation and state-of-the-art avionics. "It is capable of engaging targets with high precision and defeating the enemy's missile defence systems due to its unique trajectory and manoeuvrability features," the statement said. The military said the extended range will further enhance the army's reach and capability of engaging deep targets. ISPR did not disclose the exact range of the missile.

## The test

The launch was witnessed by the commander of the Army Rocket Force Command (ARFC), Lt Gen Nauman Zakaria, along with senior army officers and the scientists and engineers involved in the programme. The ARFC commander appreciated the dedication and technical prowess of the scientists and engineers.

President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, and the chief of defence forces congratulated the scientists and engineers on the successful launch. This was the ARFC's second missile test in October, following the test-fire of the Fatah-4 ER ground-launched cruise missile.
