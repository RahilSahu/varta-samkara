At least 13 children dead after boat capsizes in Bihar's Vaishali

At least 13 children died after a boat capsized in the Ganga river in Vaishali district of Bihar on the night of October 13, 2026, The Hindu reported. The incident took place under the Raghopur police station area of the district.

## What happened

The boat was ferrying villagers to another village, where they were to attend a feast, when it capsized on Tuesday night. The passengers on board were mostly children, and at least 13 of them lost their lives in the accident.

## Response

The report of the capsize came on Tuesday night, and the incident is one of the deadliest boat accidents in the region in recent months. Authorities in the district were informed of the tragedy following the capsize.
