Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit Named Next Chief of the Indian Air Force

The government on Tuesday appointed Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit, currently Vice Chief of the Air Staff, as the next Chief of the Air Staff. He will take charge in the rank of Air Chief Marshal from the afternoon of October 31, 2026, succeeding Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh, who superannuates the same day. His tenure will run until December 31, 2028.

Born on December 9, 1966, Dixit was commissioned into the fighter stream of the Indian Air Force on December 6, 1986. Over a career spanning nearly four decades, he has logged more than 3,500 flying hours as an experimental test pilot and qualified flying instructor, with experience on the Mirage-2000, MiG variants, Jaguar, Hawk, Kiran, IL-78 and the indigenous LCA Tejas.

## Operational and command record

His operational experience includes participation in Operation Safed Sagar, Operation Rakshak, Cope India and Operation Sindoor. He has commanded a Mirage-2000 squadron, a frontline fighter air base in the western sector and a fighter training base in the southern sector.

## Senior appointments and honours

Before becoming Vice Chief, Dixit served as Chief of Integrated Defence Staff to the Chairman, Chiefs of Staff Committee, and as Air Officer Commanding-in-Chief of Central Air Command. An alumnus of the National Defence Academy, the Defence Services Staff College in Bangladesh and the National Defence College, he has been associated closely with the indigenous LCA Tejas programme. In recognition of his service, he has been awarded the Vayu Sena Medal (2006), Vishisht Seva Medal (2011), Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (2023) and Param Vishisht Seva Medal (2026).
