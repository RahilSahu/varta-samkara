On September 29, 2026, President Donald Trump signed an executive order titled 'Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence'. It directs federal agencies to use 'Super Intelligence' and 'SI' instead of 'artificial intelligence' and 'AI' in official correspondence, websites, reports and policy documents.

"It's not artificial, we all agree on that," Trump said. He called AI "the biggest thing there is" and "much bigger than the internet", arguing the old name no longer reflects how advanced the technology has become.

The order gives Trump's science adviser 60 days to propose a formal federal definition of 'Super Intelligence'.

## A White House accord

The same day, Trump hosted Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, Sundar Pichai and Jensen Huang at a White House luncheon, where leading AI firms signed the 'White House Accord on Super Intelligence'. Trump described it as "morally binding", but it is voluntary and not legally enforceable, with four layers of safeguards and audits for advanced models.

The renaming is symbolic, but the accord signals where Washington wants the industry's safety conversation to happen: inside the White House, not just in Brussels or Geneva.
