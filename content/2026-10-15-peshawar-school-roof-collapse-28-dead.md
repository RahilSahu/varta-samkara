Peshawar school roof collapse kills at least 28, mostly students

At least 28 people, including 27 students and a female teacher, were killed after the roof of a private school collapsed in Peshawar, the capital of Pakistan's Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, on Thursday, hospital officials said.

## Rescue and arrests

Rescue operations have concluded with all trapped people recovered, according to reports. The school owner was reportedly arrested on negligence charges, and Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif took serious notice of the tragedy and ordered a report. Heavy rain has been cited as a possible factor in the collapse.

Source: Xinhua, Rooma News
