BRICS Traditional and Indigenous Demonstration Games to be held in Ahmedabad on October 12

New Delhi: The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, in collaboration with the Government of Gujarat, will organise the BRICS Traditional and Indigenous Sports Demonstration Games 2026 at the Veer Savarkar Sports Complex in Ahmedabad on October 12, under India's BRICS Chairship.

## Inauguration and dignitaries

Union Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports Mansukh Mandaviya will inaugurate the event, according to the Sports Ministry. Gujarat Deputy Chief Minister Harsh Sanghavi, Union Minister of State for Youth Affairs and Sports Raksha Nikhil Khadse, and Gujarat Minister of State for Sports Jayram Gamit are expected to attend the inauguration.

## Non-competitive platform for heritage sports

The Games will bring together delegations from BRICS member and partner countries to showcase traditional and indigenous sporting practices and promote cultural exchange, heritage preservation and people-to-people cooperation. Designed as a non-competitive platform, the event will focus on the demonstration, documentation and exchange of traditional sporting practices, with emphasis on cultural significance, practitioner interaction and knowledge-sharing. Bilateral meetings with visiting delegations are also planned on the sidelines to explore cooperation in sports within the BRICS framework.

## Sports on display

Participating countries are expected to showcase a diverse range of traditional and indigenous sports: Belarus (Gorodki), Bolivia (Lucha de Cholitas, Tinku, Rayuela, Trompo, Cachinas, Carrera de Sacos), Brazil (Zarabatana blowpipe, Corrida de Toras log race, Para-Jiu-jitsu, Capoeira), China (Wushu, Lion Dance, Health Qigong), Ethiopia (Tegel wrestling, Gena field hockey, Selus Gebeta), India (Mallakhamb, Yogasana, Kalaripayattu), Iran (Zoorkhaneh, Haft Sang, Ghanieh), Kazakhstan (Asyk Atu, Togyzkumalak, Qazaq Kuresi), Russia (Gorodki, Sambo), South Africa (Kgati, Iintonga) and the United Arab Emirates (Al Tabbah).
