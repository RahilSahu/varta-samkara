Houthis strike Saudi Aramco headquarters in overnight attack

Iran-backed Houthi rebels hit the headquarters of the Saudi state-owned oil company Aramco in an overnight strike, two regional officials said on Wednesday, damaging the offices of the firm's chief executive and other top officials.

## Escalating energy infrastructure attacks

The strike follows a pattern of attacks on Gulf energy infrastructure amid heightened regional tensions. On Thursday, the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen said it had intercepted and destroyed a cruise missile in the Medina region.

Source: The Hindu, Xinhua
