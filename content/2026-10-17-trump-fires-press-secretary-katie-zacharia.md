Trump Fires Press Secretary Katie Zacharia Just Five Days Into the Job

US President Donald Trump has fired White House press secretary Katie Zacharia on Friday, just five days after she started the role, following a turbulent first week at the podium.

## A rocky week

Zacharia's tenure began with a difficult first press briefing on Tuesday, a 14-minute appearance in which she repeatedly stumbled over a script and took questions from only three conservative-friendly outlets. Questions then mounted over her legal credentials, with reporting that she had never passed a bar exam despite having been described as a lawyer when Trump announced her appointment.

Trump told reporters on Friday that he had asked Zacharia to "reconsider" whether she wanted the job. "I said, 'Honestly, if I were you, I'd reconsider,'" he said, adding that she had been treated roughly by the media. "Maybe it's not for her."

## Aftermath

Hours after her ouster, Zacharia wrote on social media that "it was an honor" to work for Trump, calling him "kind, wonderful, gracious, thoughtful" and hardworking. She had replaced her predecessor Karolina Levitt, who had served in the post for over a year and a half.

Zacharia's five-day stint is the shortest of any top Trump White House aide, beating the record set by first-term communications director Anthony Scaramucci, whose 11-day tenure had become an informal unit of time.
