Abhishek Sharma and Arshdeep Singh Power India to 14 Run Win Over West Indies in Third T20I

India beat the West Indies by 14 runs in the third T20I in Indore to take a 2 to 1 lead in the five match series, defending a total of 223.

## Match summary

Abhishek Sharma scored 69 off 30 balls and shared a century opening stand with Ishan Kishan, who made 37, as India posted 223 all out despite a middle overs slide. In reply, the West Indies finished at 209 for seven.

## Bowling and chase

Arshdeep Singh took 3 for 44 and was named player of the match. He dismissed captain Shai Hope for 5 early, and later removed Sherfane Rutherford and Keemo Paul. Naman Dhir took 2 for 39. Shimron Hetmyer made 43 and Kamil Pooran 42 for the visitors, while Shamar Springer late hitting was not enough to complete the chase.
