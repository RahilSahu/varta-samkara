CJP Founder Abhijeet Dipke Granted Conditional Bail After Arrest Over Peace-Bond Violation

Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) founder Abhijeet Dipke was granted conditional bail on Saturday, a day after he was detained on his way to a protest in central Delhi.

## The detention and arrest

Dipke was detained on Friday moments after he stepped out of his house in Delhi's Jhandewalan area to participate in the CJP's "Lehra Do Tiranga" protest at Connaught Place. He was arrested on Saturday morning for allegedly violating a peace bond he had furnished on October 10.

## Bail conditions

The special executive magistrate released Dipke after he signed a conditional bail bond. One of the primary conditions is that he must ensure there is no breach of peace on his part for a period of one year.

The detention had drawn attention amid ongoing anti-CEC protests in the capital over electoral roll revisions, which the CJP movement has been leading.
