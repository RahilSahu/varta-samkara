I love my country. India is the best. Not because it is perfect, not because it leads every ranking. Because it is my home.

Say that out loud today and watch what happens. Some people will nod. Others will smirk, pull up a statistic, and ask you which ranking exactly you are referring to. As if love needed a citation.

## "India is the best" was never a statistical claim

"India is the best country" is not a statistical statement. It is an emotional one, like saying your parents are the best in the world. Nobody who says that means their parents topped some global parenting index. They mean: these are mine, I love them, and that love is not up for peer review.

A homeland is different from a "best city in the world" list. You do not love your home because it scored well. You love it because it made you. The language you dream in, the food that tastes like childhood, the festivals, the chaos, the familiarity of it all, that is not a ranking. That is belonging.

## Criticizing the government is healthy. Undermining the country is not.

Criticism of the government is necessary in a democracy. It is not just allowed, it is the job. A government that cannot be questioned is a government that cannot be corrected.

But somewhere along the way, some crossed the line from criticizing a government to undermining their own country. There is a difference between "this policy is wrong" and "I hope this fails so my side wins." There is a difference between holding power accountable and cheering when the nation stumbles.

National crises should bring concern and solidarity, not political excitement. When the country faces a real threat, a real tragedy, a real emergency, that is not the moment for "I told you so." That is the moment to stand together first and argue later.

## Wanting your own country to fail is self-destructive

Think about it practically. If you live here, your future is tied to this country's future. Your job, your savings, your children's prospects, all of it rides on India doing well. Hoping the country fails to prove a political point is like setting fire to your own house to prove the landlord is negligent. You might be right about the landlord. You will still be homeless.

You can hate a government. You can campaign against it, vote against it, write against it every single day. But the nation is bigger than any government, and it will outlast every single one of them.

## So yes, I will keep saying it

India is the best. Not the richest, not the most powerful, not the most perfect. The best, because it is mine.

Saare jahan se accha, Hindustan humara.
