YourStory launches ShuruKar in Patna to take entrepreneurship to district level

Patna: The Bharat Project, an initiative by YourStory, on Friday launched ShuruKar at the Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Science City in Rajinder Nagar, Patna, billing it as the first nationwide district-level entrepreneurship movement of its kind, developed in partnership with DPIIT and Startup India.

## What ShuruKar aims to do

ShuruKar is designed to help young Indians identify a problem worth solving in their own district, build a business around it and grow it, moving participants through four stages: finding the problem, shaping the idea with an AI copilot, building with mentor support until the first customer is won, and tapping district-level markets and networks. The platform is intended to work through existing government structures without creating a new scheme or seeking government funding. The launch also announced the ShuruKar Challenge, inviting solutions to major national problems.

## Voices from the launch

The launch drew Bihar's Urban Development and Housing Minister Nitish Mishra, along with founders, students and young entrepreneurs from across the state. Shradha Sharma, Founder and CEO of YourStory and Founder of The Bharat Project, said the initiative marks a shift from documenting entrepreneurs to creating them, arguing that talent and ambition are everywhere in India but access is not. Sharma said India's next generation of founders will come as much from Patna, Gaya, Darbhanga and Muzaffarpur as from the metros.

Minister Mishra said perceptions of Bihar lag behind reality, adding that the state government is promoting entrepreneurship and new businesses and that young people should make full use of available support. Keynotes at the event were delivered by Amar Dixit, CEO of WOI India, and Johnson Tellis, Co-Founder and CEO of InUnity.
