Meerut court sentences two to death in Blue Drum murder case

A local court in Meerut on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, sentenced Muskan Rastogi and her partner Sahil Shukla to death in the high-profile Blue Drum murder case, The Hindu reported.

## The case

Muskan allegedly murdered her husband, Saurabh Rastogi, with the help of Shukla on March 3, 2025. According to the case details, the victim was first rendered unconscious using sedatives. The accused then allegedly dismembered his body, placed the remains inside a blue plastic drum, and sealed it with cement to conceal the crime.

## The verdict

The court's death sentences bring one of the most closely watched murder trials of recent years to a close. The case had drawn nationwide attention because of the manner in which the body was concealed.
