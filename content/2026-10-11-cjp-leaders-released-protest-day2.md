CJP Leaders Released as Anti CEC Protest Enters Second Day in Delhi

Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke and co convenors Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka were released on Sunday, October 11, by an executive magistrate a day after they were detained in Delhi ahead of a protest seeking the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Reports said their release was on the condition that they maintain peace for one year.

The group vowed to continue its agitation over alleged irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls. Stringent traffic restrictions remained in force for a second day around Jantar Mantar, Connaught Place, Mandi House and Patel Chowk, with diversions enforced from 6 a.m., the Delhi Traffic Police said.

## Political responses

Delhi Police released a video showing an officer informing Mr Dipke about the death of his grandmother and offering transport for the funeral and a call to family. Mr Dipke is seen declining and saying he would stay. CJP leaders criticised the police handling of the episode, while the BJP criticised the CJP for shifting from student issues to electoral politics.

The Congress said it would launch a voter awareness drive in Delhi amid the SIR protest. Congress leader Rahul Gandhi said the INDIA bloc would hold a protest march in Delhi on Monday, October 12, and that a delegation would submit a memorandum to the Chief Justice of India. Aaditya Thackeray and Amit Thackeray protested in Mumbai against the detention of demonstrators there, and fresh protests were announced in Mumbai and Lucknow.

## Detentions on Saturday

Police said permission for the Jantar Mantar protest had been denied. Around 3,000 people were detained across Delhi on Saturday, according to reports, including opposition leaders. Delhi Police said more than 20 FIRs were registered over Saturday protests. The INDIA bloc and the CJP said detentions continued on Sunday in parts of the capital.
