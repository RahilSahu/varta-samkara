Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, was added to the FBI's Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list on September 28, becoming the 543rd fugitive on the historic list. The reward for information leading to his arrest jumped from $50,000 to $1,000,000 (about Rs 9.58 crore).

The FBI says Brar is the alleged North American leader of the Lawrence Bishnoi Organised Crime Group, linked to assassinations, shootings, kidnappings, extortion and drug trafficking across the US and Canada. He faces RICO conspiracy, extortion and narcotics charges, with a federal arrest warrant from the US District Court in California.

The FBI's warning is blunt: consider him armed, dangerous and an escape risk.

## Why this matters

Placement on the Ten Most Wanted list puts Brar in the most visible category of American fugitives, with the million-dollar reward designed to shake loose information from anyone who has seen him. For Indian law enforcement, which has sought him in connection with high-profile killings, the listing marks a significant escalation in the international hunt.
