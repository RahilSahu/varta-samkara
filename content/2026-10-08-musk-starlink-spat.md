Government Rejects Elon Musk's Starlink Allegations as Rahul Gandhi Joins the Debate

The government on Thursday firmly rejected Elon Musk's claim that Starlink's satellite internet services were being blocked in India, with the Ministry of Communications calling the allegations "baseless and misconceived" and asserting that its framework was "fair and non-discriminatory".

Musk escalated the row on X, questioning the delay in Starlink's India commercial licence and asking, "Is Ambani the real boss of India?", referring to Reliance Jio chief Mukesh Ambani. He claimed Starlink had spent five years complying with Indian requirements and earlier alleged that unnamed oligarchs were blocking the service. Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi joined the exchange, replying to Musk: "Welcome to India, Elon. Wait till you discover the other guy."

## Security clearance pending with Home Ministry

The government said all three satellite service licensees, including Starlink, were at broadly the same regulatory stage with security assessments under way, rejecting any discrimination. Minister of State for Communications said the Ministry of Home Affairs is looking into security-related issues with satcom services, noting that Starlink's security clearance remains pending with the Home Ministry. The ministry stressed that the same rules applied equally to all operators, directly countering Musk's claims of special treatment for rivals.
