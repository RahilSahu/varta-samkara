INDIA Bloc Marches to Supreme Court and Files Petition Against Election Chief Over SIR
Opposition leaders led by Rahul Gandhi marched in central Delhi on Monday and moved the Supreme Court against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, challenging large scale voter deletions and alleged irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls and seeking his removal.
## What happened in Delhi
Leaders and MPs gathered at the Constitution Club and proceeded towards Barakhamba Road and the Supreme Court on Tilak Marg. Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge and Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav were among those who took part, along with Priyanka Gandhi Vadra and student leader Neha Bora. Entry and exit at six Metro stations, Patel Chowk, Janpath, Rajiv Chowk, Mandi House, Central Secretariat and Barakhamba Road, were closed from 6 a.m. to 4 p.m., the DMRC said. Police said Jantar Mantar had been denotified as a designated protest site, with Ramlila Maidan, Burari Ground and CBD Ground named as new venues.
## The court route
Chief Justice of India Surya Kant had declined a request for a private meeting, citing matters already pending before the court, and said petitioners could approach the court on its judicial side. Related matters are listed for hearing on Tuesday, October 13. The Chief Justice said the court remains open to all and that concerns would be addressed in accordance with law. The Supreme Court Bar Association plea on detention of lawyers and internet restrictions is also listed for Tuesday.
## Responses
BJP leader and Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan criticised the march and said the Congress was targeting constitutional institutions after electoral defeats. BJP MP Eatala Rajender urged the Opposition to place evidence before the Election Commission. The Election Commission has not issued a fresh statement on the petition in the reports reviewed for this article.
