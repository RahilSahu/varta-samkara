PM Modi and CM Vijay Flag Off First Train of Chennai Metro Phase 2

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay on Sunday, October 11, flagged off the first train of Chennai Metro Rail Phase 2 from Porur Bypass station and rode together to Poonamallee. With the opening of the Poonamallee to Vadapalani stretch, the city Metro network grew from 54 km to 68 km, Chennai Metro Rail Limited said.

The stretch is the first section of the 118.9 km Phase 2 network, work on which began nearly six years ago. Passenger services on the new stretch began at 4 p.m. on Sunday. Regular services from Monday will run from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m., with 15 trains operating at intervals of 10 minutes in peak hours and 15 minutes at other times in the initial period, officials said.

## Corridor details

The Indian Express reported that the roughly 15 km stretch, built at a cost of around Rs 4,900 crore, has 11 elevated stations and forms part of Corridor 4. It includes a 3.7 km double decker structure and signalling designed to support driverless operations, and is expected to improve connectivity between western suburbs and the city centre.

After the flag off, the Prime Minister was scheduled to inaugurate and lay foundation stones for development projects worth more than Rs 13,000 crore at an event linked to IIT Madras, covering highways, railways, ports, petroleum and education. Mr Modi said in Chennai that development is his mantra and that he would work with the Chief Minister for Tamil Nadu growth, PTI reported.

## Why timings are shorter for now

Officials said Phase 2 timings are initially shorter than Phase 1 timings of 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. to allow night construction at six stations between Porur Junction and Vadapalani. Frequency is expected to improve once that civil work is completed.
