Andhra Pradesh Declares Drought in 21 Districts Covering 388 Mandals After 53% Rainfall Deficit

The Andhra Pradesh government has declared 388 of its 688 mandals, across 21 of the state's 26 districts, as drought-affected for the South-West Monsoon 2026 Kharif season, with 283 classified as severely affected and 105 as moderately affected. The Revenue (Disaster Management) Department issued the order on October 1 after a high-level Committee on Drought recommended an early declaration on September 30, rather than waiting for the October 31 deadline.

The order cites a 53.4 per cent rainfall deficit during June-August 2026, with average rainfall of 197.6 mm against the normal 424.3 mm, amplified by prevailing El Nino conditions. The declaration follows the Government of India's Manual for Drought Management, 2016. The affected districts include Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, Anakapalli, Kakinada, East Godavari, Eluru, Krishna, NTR district, Guntur, Bapatla, Palnadu, Prakasam, Markapuram, Kurnool, Nandyal, Ananthapuramu, Sri Satya Sai, YSR Kadapa, Annamayya and Chittoor.

## Opposition questions delay and Nellore exclusion

The YSRCP slammed the government for declaring drought only now, alleging conditions turned severe in the first week of September, and for excluding Nellore district despite low rainfall in several of its mandals. YSRCP leaders said Rayalaseema bore the brunt, with 186 of the 388 affected mandals and 159 of the 283 severe-drought mandals in the region, claiming nearly seven lakh borewells dried up by August. Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu responded that 2,000 TMC of river water flows into the sea each year and said utilising at least 200 TMC of Godavari floodwater could make Rayalaseema drought-free through river interlinking.
